## Product Overview

**One-liner:** MASEST supplies VertKleen industrial cleaning products with help choosing, testing, and procuring the right product for the job.
**What it does:** Connects cleaning applications to products, available small-pack purchases, bulk inquiries, samples, documents, field support, and water-system services.
**Product category:** Industrial cleaning chemistry and application support.
**Product type:** Physical products plus services; the website supports procurement.
**Business model:** Online purchases for available packs; quotes for bulk supply, private label, distributor relationships, and services. Public prices and availability must come from the commerce catalog.

## Target Audience

**Target companies:** Mechanical contractors, facility maintenance teams, industrial cleaning businesses, cleaning/restoration companies, water-treatment providers, resellers, and distributors. These are website-supported segments, not measured best-performing audiences.
**Decision-makers:** Candidate roles include maintenance managers, contractor owners, technical reviewers, purchasing staff, and distributor buyers. Validate roles through customer interviews.
**Primary use case:** Identify the soil and substrate, review product documents, test suitability, and buy or request a scoped quote.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Select chemistry for a defined cleaning job and equipment boundary.
- Compare a complete cleaning cycle, including preparation, rinsing, labor, downtime, and disposal.
- Obtain current labels, SDS, packs, pricing, and product help.
- Explore existing VertKleen products supplied with the customer's branding.

## Personas

- **Maintenance or service lead:** Needs a practical product choice and operating boundaries. Support with application guidance, current documents, and a site-specific trial.
- **Purchasing or technical reviewer:** Needs packs, price, documentation, and evidence. Support with the current catalog and a scoped quote.
- **Distributor or private-label buyer:** Needs product fit, packaging, branding options, and commercial terms. Obtain those through qualification and quotation; do not promise unconfirmed terms.

These role descriptions are hypotheses, not interview-derived personas.

## Problems & Pain Points

Website-supported problems: scale, rust, grease, organic residue, general grime, odor, and water-system buildup. Candidate procurement concerns: uncertain product fit, material compatibility, cleaning-cycle cost, documentation, and operational downtime.

Pain intensity, buyer phrasing, willingness to switch, and purchase frequency remain unmeasured.

## Competitive Landscape

Evaluate alternatives by exact product and application. Current comparison routes address RYDLYME, CLR, Simple Green, Wet & Forget, and brewery cleaning-cycle costs. Do not treat a brand-wide statement as an exact product comparison. Broader private-label alternatives include contract suppliers and cleaning-chemical distributors; validate commercial capabilities through primary sources.

## Differentiation

Communicate product selection, current documents, practical trials, published job records, small-pack procurement, and human application support. Use chemistry, hazard, efficacy, environmental, and shipping claims only with product-specific approved evidence. Do not infer competitor inferiority or universal superiority from one field result.

## Objections & Anti-Personas

Questions to answer: Will this suit my equipment and soil? What does the current label/SDS require? What trial would demonstrate fit? What is the complete cycle cost? Which packs and terms are available? What private-label scope is supported?

Do not qualify prospects using invented criteria. Requests outside supported applications need technical review rather than stronger marketing promises.

## Switching Dynamics

Hypotheses: approved chemical lists, trained crews, existing supplier relationships, and shutdown schedules may slow switching. Test a defined job against the customer's current method before recommending a broader change. Do not promise savings without a measured baseline.

## Customer Language

No verified voice-of-customer transcript corpus supplied. Existing website language includes product help, quotes, samples, labels/SDS, field records, private label, scale/rust removal, and cleaning-cycle cost. Treat website copy separately from customer quotations.

## Brand Voice

Plain, specific, technically credible, and useful to working buyers. Name the job and next action. Prefer evidence and operating boundaries over broad safety language, unsupported superlatives, or generic growth claims.
