VertKleen LAM3 vs Wet & Forget: compare the finished area

MASEST Technical Team · July 9, 2026 · 2 min read

Exterior cleaning is about time and area

Moss, algae, mildew, lichen, and dark staining do not leave every surface at the same speed.

Shade, moisture, porosity, weather, and growth depth all change the result. That makes cost per finished area more useful than bottle price.

How Wet & Forget works

Wet & Forget tells users to dilute its concentrate 1:5, saturate a dry surface, and let weather remove the stains over time.

The company describes it as a no-scrub, no-rinse treatment and says results may take days to months.

That gradual approach can fit properties where an immediate finish is not the priority.

See Wet & Forget's use directionshttps://www.wetandforget.com/wet-and-forget-concentrate.html

How LAM3 approaches the job

VertKleen LAM3 stays wet longer across moss, algae, mildew, lichen, and visible exterior staining.

Purgo supports the program by continuing to work on the organic buildup after application.

Together, they give contractors a long-dwell approach with an HMIS 0-0-0 profile.

Compare equal areas

Choose two areas with the same surface, staining, shade, weather exposure, and drainage.

Photograph them from the same spot and compare coverage, product used, labor, runoff, visible change, return visits, and time to the desired finish.

Let the result develop

MASEST has LAM3 and Purgo results on a painted exterior column with visible improvement after two weeks.

That is the kind of job where progress photos matter. The value appears across the treatment period, not in a rushed same-minute reveal.

Use the same photo angle and inspection days for both products so the finished-area comparison stays clear.

HMIS 0-0-0 for recurring exterior work

LAM3 and Purgo are HMIS 0-0-0, like every VertKleen product MASEST offers.

That can simplify storage, technician handling, and property coordination for work that repeats across seasons and sites.

Use each product as directed, protect nearby surfaces and landscaping, and begin with a small test area.
