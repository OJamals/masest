LAM3 vs Wet & Forget

MASEST Technical Team · July 9, 2026 · 1 min read

Wetting and dwell do the work

VertKleen LAM3 extends wetting and dwell across moss, algae, mildew, lichen, and visible exterior staining. Purgo support continues working on the organic matrix after application, reducing dependence on a bleach-first maintenance cycle.

Compare the finished area

Record surface type, staining, dilution, coverage, dwell, material and landscape experience, labor, rinse, and maintenance interval. Before-and-after exterior records show the visible endpoint to compare.

Control the field trial

Choose equal test areas with the same substrate, stain, shade, weather, and drainage. Photograph the baseline from marked positions. Record application volume, wet dwell, runoff handling, visible change, adjacent-material condition, labor, and inspection date. Use the same endpoint for both products.

Price by treated area

LAM3 lists at $22.21/gal; Wet & Forget is listed at $34.00/gal in the comparison source. Apply dilution and coverage to calculate cost per treated area, then add labor and repeat frequency.
