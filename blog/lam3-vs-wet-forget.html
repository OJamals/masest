VertKleen LAM3 vs Wet & Forget: compare the finished area

MASEST Technical Team · July 9, 2026 · 3 min read

Exterior cleaning is a time-and-area problem

Moss, algae, mildew, lichen, and visible staining do not leave every surface on the same clock. Shade, moisture, porosity, weather, and growth depth change the result.

That makes bottle price a weak comparison. Measure treated area, labor, visible change, adjacent-material condition, and maintenance interval.

How Wet & Forget is designed to work

Wet & Forget directs users to dilute its concentrate 1:5, saturate a dry surface, and let weathering remove stains over time. Its site says results may take days to months.

The company positions it as a no-scrub, no-rinse treatment with annual use or reapplication as needed. Those are useful strengths when a gradual result fits the property.

Review Wet & Forget’s current use directionshttps://www.wetandforget.com/wet-and-forget-concentrate.html

How LAM3 approaches the same outcome

VertKleen LAM3 extends wetting and dwell across exterior organic growth and visible staining. Purgo support continues working on the organic matrix after application.

The practical question is how that program changes the finished area, labor, runoff, return visit, and maintenance interval on the actual substrate.

Compare equal test areas

Choose matched areas with the same substrate, stain, shade, weather, slope, and drainage. Mark camera positions and photograph the baseline.

Record mixed volume, coverage, wet dwell, runoff handling, visible change, adjacent materials, labor, and inspection dates. Judge both areas on the same day and endpoint.

Use field evidence, not instant-result theater

MASEST records LAM3/Purgo work on a painted exterior column with visible improvement after two weeks. That supports a long-dwell program, not an artificial same-minute reveal.

A fair trial should photograph the same area over the interval promised by each method. It should also record any brushing, rinsing, or repeat application.

Add the HMIS 0-0-0 difference

Every offered VertKleen product is HMIS 0-0-0, including LAM3 and Purgo. That can simplify storage, technician handling, and property coordination around recurring exterior work.

Follow the current label, SDS, dilution, runoff plan, landscape protections, and substrate guidance. Test a small area before broad application.
