Commercial kitchen degreasing: cut through baked-on grease before reopening

MASEST Team · August 3, 2026 · 3 min read

Grease keeps working after the burners stop

Heat turns cooking oil into a sticky film across backsplashes, hoods, filters, floors, and the cookline. Miss a few closes and the next cleanup becomes a much bigger job.

The best closing plan gives heavy grease time to loosen, keeps every step in order, and leaves the line ready for the next shift.

Match the cleaner to the kitchen job

CR HD is built for baked-on grease, oil, and carbon around the cookline. CR HD Low Foam brings the same heavy-duty approach to floor scrubbers and other recovery equipment.

Neutral fits the lighter finish work after the hard dirt is gone. Check the latest label and SDS for each kitchen product before use.

Run the close in five steps

Clear and cool the line. Apply CR HD to the greasy area. Give it time to work, scrub the stubborn spots, then rinse, dry, and inspect.

One order keeps crews from spraying over food, working around hot equipment, or cleaning the same surface twice.

Let working time replace repeat passes

Heavy grease rarely needs more frantic scrubbing. It needs enough cleaner, even coverage, and time for the dirt to release.

Start on one clearly marked section. When the grease lifts cleanly, carry that product use and working time across the rest of the line.
