Get the cookline ready for morning prep

Heat turns cooking oil into a bonded film on hood filters, backsplashes, fryer legs, and the floor edge behind the range. The closing crew has to reach those collection points before the opening crew rolls carts and ingredients back onto the line.

A productive close cools and clears the line, gives the degreaser time to work, and keeps the final rinse and sanitation steps moving in order.

Run a five-stage close

Before: baked-on grease on the Fort Lauderdale cookline.

After: the cleaned cookline following CR HD treatment. See the full result.

Clear food and removable items. Let hot surfaces reach the permitted cleaning temperature. Apply evenly, allow working time, agitate only where needed, then rinse and inspect.

Complete the kitchen’s required food-contact sanitation and release steps after cleaning. A degreaser removes soil; it does not replace the approved sanitation program.

Use working time to reduce repeat passes

On baked grease, immediate scrubbing wastes chemistry and labor. Start at the heaviest end of the line so CR HD is working while the crew handles lighter areas.

A deliberate sequence can reduce second passes and shorten the interval between shutdown and a ready cookline.
