How VertKleen Descaler restored a scaled fire pump at a Walmart DC

MASEST Technical Team · August 3, 2026 · 3 min read

A routine check found a pump that would not start

At a Walmart distribution center in Cocoa, Florida, Siemens fire-protection technicians arrived for planned maintenance and found a fire pump that would not start.

This was not a cosmetic cleaning job. The pump had to move water at the right flow and pressure, and the first sign of trouble was already stopping it from running.

Rust and calcite had locked up the solenoid

The solenoid switch was covered with rust and calcite. The technicians applied VertKleen Descaler for 30 minutes, wiped the part clean, and reinstalled it.

The pump started immediately. That quick win exposed the next problem instead of hiding it.

The pump started, but the numbers stayed low

Water flow and pressure were still below target. The team opened the pump and found calcite covering the cavity.

Cleaning the switch solved the no-start. Restoring pump performance meant reaching the buildup deeper inside the system.

The cavity needed the deeper clean

Following the plan from Siemens and MASEST, the team filled the cavity with Descaler and left it for 24 hours. The next day, they cleaned, rinsed, and reassembled the pump.

The sequence matters: start with the visible restriction, test the equipment, follow the numbers, then clean the remaining deposit.

A 30-minute test confirmed the recovery

After reassembly, the team ran the pump for 30 minutes. Proper water flow and pressure returned.

VertKleen Descaler is acid-free, non-caustic, mild in odor, HMIS 0-0-0, and non-regulated for transport. It removes calcium, lime, scale, and rust without bringing hydrochloric acid into the job.

In a controlled calcium-capacity test, Descaler held 15% more calcium than muriatic acid. It brings serious descaling power with a much easier product profile.

Stop scale before it becomes another no-start

Water left in a pump cavity gives mineral deposits time to grow. The case team recommended adding a descaling wash every three months instead of waiting for another lockup.

Planned cleaning turns an urgent repair into a short, repeatable maintenance task with a visible condition check and a flow-and-pressure test at the end.
