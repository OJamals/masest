How to descale a heat exchanger and get it back online faster

MASEST Technical Team · August 3, 2026 · 3 min read

Scale steals time twice

Scale narrows flow and coats the surfaces that move heat. Then the cleanup takes the unit offline.

A better plan removes the buildup and gets the heat exchanger working again without turning one maintenance window into a marathon.

Find the buildup before choosing the cleaner

Hard-water scale, rust, process residue, oil, and organic film do not respond to the same chemistry.

If oily film covers mineral scale, clean the oil first with CR or CR HD. Then let HCR reach the hard-water buildup underneath.

Build one clean recirculation loop

Connect the isolated heat exchanger to a pump and reservoir. Keep the return visible so the crew can watch what comes back from the plates or tubes.

StageWhat the crew doesWhat to watch
IsolateTake the unit offline and drain itValves, volume, and access
ConnectSet the pump, reservoir, and hosesSecure flow path
CirculateMove HCR through the fouled sideReturn flow and released buildup
RinseFlush the loosened scale awayClear return
InspectCheck plates, tubes, pressure, and flowClean surface and working system

The loop keeps the work contained and puts every step in the order the crew will run it.

Why HCR changes the descaling job

VertKleen HCR breaks down carbonate scale and rust with an HMIS 0-0-0 profile. Every current VertKleen product MASEST offers carries the same rating.

HCR measured only 0.59 mpy on steel—up to 280× less corrosion than hydrochloric acid—with 15% more calcium-carbonate capacity than HCl.

That gives crews strong scale removal while protecting more of the equipment value underneath the buildup.

Fast chemistry matters. A fast restart matters more.

Count setup, circulation, rinse water, inspection, repeat passes, and the time from isolation until the system is running again.
