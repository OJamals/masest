Scale charges rent every hour it stays in the exchanger

Mineral buildup narrows passages and insulates the surfaces that transfer heat.

The unit runs longer, flow declines, and the eventual shutdown becomes more expensive. A disciplined recirculation clean can recover performance without spending equipment life on harsh acid.

Identify the layer touching the metal

Hard-water scale and rust call for HCR. Oil or organic film can block HCR from reaching that deposit.

When the return shows mixed soil, remove the grease first with CR or CR HD, rinse, and then begin the mineral stage.

Build a visible recirculation loop

Isolate and drain the exchanger. Connect a compatible pump, reservoir, hoses, and fittings so the return remains visible.

Compare the pressure drop and outlet temperatures with the clean baseline at similar flow and load. A loaded upstream strainer can resemble exchanger fouling; check it before extending the chemical cycle. Alfa Laval's operating guidance identifies rising pressure drop and falling thermal performance as cleaning signals, before blocked passages prevent useful circulation.

HCR removes scale while respecting the asset

Source testing measured HCR at 0.59 mpy on steel, up to 280 times less corrosion than hydrochloric acid.

The same comparison measured 15% more calcium-carbonate capacity than HCl.

That combination targets the deposit while preserving more of the exchanger value under it.

A simpler product helps the shutdown stay on schedule

HCR is HMIS 0-0-0, non-fuming, ships non-hazmat, and adds no product-specific PPE or ventilation requirement for routine use.

Isolation, pressure, temperature, gas release, electrical equipment, confined access, and wastewater still shape the job controls.

The advantage is powerful descaling without adding an acid-handling workflow to those existing controls.

Brevard shows what effective cleaning can change

At a Brevard County School District HVAC system, CLR remained on a 20-year stainless-steel plate and drain for 36 hours without clearing the rust.

DDC Engineering applied HCR for 30 minutes and rinsed with a garden hose. No scrubbing was recorded.

Close the job with operating data

Record product, circulation, rinse water, labor, total downtime, and the exchanger readings before and after cleaning.

A clean plate is useful proof. Restored flow and heat transfer complete the business case.
