Heavy grease does not always call for harsh alkalinity

Oil reaches painted panels, rubber seals, stainless trim, hoses, wheels, and finished floors.

A cleaner that removes the grease but damages the surrounding material creates rework. VertKleen Neutral delivers industrial degreasing at pH 7.5.

Neutral handles a wider mix of surfaces

VertKleen Neutral lifts petroleum oil, animal and vegetable fats, dirt, soot, and protein soil without butyl or solvents.

That makes it useful where frequent cleaning, seals, coatings, and mixed materials matter as much as raw grease removal.

JobStarting dilution
Heavy degreasing3:1

Use the concentration the dirt needs. Moving lighter work to 30:1 or 40:1 protects margin and makes one concentrate useful across more of the facility.

One product can follow the maintenance crew

Neutral works with hot or cold water in parts washers, pressure-cleaning equipment, floor care, shop cleaning, restaurant degreasing, and general facility work.

That range reduces product changes during the shift and replaces overlapping cleaners in the cabinet.

Keep grease easier to separate

Many degreasers emulsify animal and vegetable fats into the wash water.

Neutral lifts those fats without the same emulsifying action, helping the grease remain easier to separate rather than pushing the cleaning problem toward the trap.

For kitchens and food plants, that can reduce downstream maintenance while still leaving the site’s sanitation process intact.

Daily handling stays straightforward

Neutral is HMIS 0-0-0, mild in odor, non-flammable, and non-hazmat for shipping.

Routine use adds no product-specific PPE or ventilation requirement. The work itself may still require protection for hot equipment, pressure spray, food soil, moving machinery, or slippery floors.

Price each dilution by completed work

A gallon used at 3:1 and a gallon used at 40:1 create very different amounts of working solution.

Calculate product per finished part, machine, or square foot. Add labor, water, repeat passes, and surface rework.

That is where pH 7.5 and a broad dilution range become operating advantages rather than label facts.
