Neutral-pH industrial degreaser: clean oil without harsh caustics

MASEST Technical Team · August 3, 2026 · 3 min read

Oil rarely lands on bare steel alone

Grease creeps across painted panels, rubber seals, stainless trim, hoses, wheels, and controls. A cleaner that attacks the oil but punishes the surrounding materials creates a second job.

VertKleen Neutral gives maintenance teams a practical middle path: industrial degreasing power at pH 7.5.

Why neutral pH opens up more of the facility

VertKleen Neutral lifts petroleum oil, animal and vegetable fats, dirt, soot, and protein soil without butyl or solvents.

That makes it a strong fit when finishes, seals, mixed materials, or frequent cleaning matter as much as the grease itself.

Match the dilution to the dirt

Use the strongest label mix for the hardest soil, not for every surface by default. The right dilution saves product and makes the result easier to repeat.

A 3:1 mix handles heavy degreasing. Move to 10:1 for normal degreasing, 30:1 for general cleaning, and 40:1 for floors.

One cleaner can cover more of the workday

Neutral can move from oily equipment to floors, parts washers, pressure-cleaning equipment, shop areas, restaurant degreasing, and general facility cleaning. It works with hot or cold water.

That range matters for teams trying to shrink an overfilled chemical cabinet without settling for a weak everyday cleaner.

Keep grease traps from becoming part of the problem

Many degreasers emulsify fat into the wash. VertKleen Neutral lifts animal and vegetable fats without that same emulsifying action, so the grease stays easier to separate.

For kitchens and food plants, that can reduce the amount of cleaning trouble pushed downstream toward the grease trap.

Prove it on one dirty panel

Choose a surface that represents the real job: oily paint, a rubber seal, stainless trim, or a floor near the equipment. Mark a test patch and photograph the starting condition.

Mix for the soil, apply, brush or wipe, rinse with fresh water, then inspect the dry surface. Record the dilution, contact time, effort, and finish.

That small test gives operators and purchasing one result they can judge together.

HMIS 0-0-0 fits a cleaner used often

VertKleen Neutral is HMIS 0-0-0, non-flammable, mild in odor, and non-regulated for transport. Every current VertKleen product MASEST offers carries the same HMIS 0-0-0 profile.

A frequently used cleaner touches receiving, storage, training, daily handling, and reorder planning. A simpler product profile pays off every time the bottle comes off the shelf.
