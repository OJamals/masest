How VertKleen removes scale without a conventional mineral-acid workflow

MASEST Technical Team · June 20, 2026 · 1 min read

The efficiency tax of scale

Carbonate scale narrows flow paths and insulates heat-transfer surfaces. The cleaning job is to release that deposit without making uncontrolled mineral-acid attack the operating method.

Controlled mineral removal

VertKleen HCR and Descaler use controlled hydrogen-ion activity to consume carbonate scale: 2 H+ + CaCO3 → Ca2+ + CO2 + H2O. Complexing, wetting, and inhibition keep released material moving toward rinse-out while supporting equipment-conscious work.

Match concentration, circulation, contact time, and rinse to the deposit and system.

Plan the circulation job

Record system volume, metallurgy, deposit location, available flow, temperature, concentration, contact time, gas release, rinse endpoint, wastewater path, and restart criteria. Isolate a representative section or coupon when possible. Compare deposit release, surface condition, flow recovery, water, crew time, and full shutdown duration against the current method.

Use the VertKleen handling profile

Current documentation records HMIS 0-0-0, non-hazmat shipping, and no special ventilation or area-clearance requirement for routine use. Eye or skin contact may be mildly irritating, without chemical burns or permanent damage. Follow the current product label and SDS.
