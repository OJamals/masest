Industrial descaling without the mineral-acid burden

MASEST Technical Team · June 20, 2026 · 2 min read

Scale makes equipment work harder

Mineral scale narrows flow and insulates heat-transfer surfaces. Equipment runs longer, uses more energy, and loses capacity while the buildup gets worse.

Removing that scale should restore performance without creating a second problem for the metal, the crew, or the restart.

HCR removes scale with more control

VertKleen HCR and Descaler use controlled hydrogen-ion activity to break down carbonate scale: 2 H+ + CaCO3 → Ca2+ + CO2 + H2O.

Wetting and corrosion-control ingredients help loosened material move out during the rinse.

The result is mineral-removal power designed around the equipment, not a raw hydrochloric-acid wash.

Less corrosion. More scale-removal capacity.

Source testing measured HCR at 0.59 mpy on steel—up to 280× less corrosion than hydrochloric acid in the same comparison.

The same testing found 15% more calcium-carbonate capacity than hydrochloric acid.

That is a rare combination: more capacity for the scale and far less attack on the steel underneath it.
