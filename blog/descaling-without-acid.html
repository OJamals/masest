Mineral scale is an operating expense

Scale narrows flow and insulates heat-transfer surfaces. Pumps work harder, temperatures drift, and production capacity falls.

Removing the deposit should recover performance without spending equipment life on an aggressive mineral-acid wash.

HCR delivers a different tradeoff

VertKleen HCR uses synthetic acid-replacement chemistry to dissolve carbonate scale and release rust.

Source testing measured steel corrosion at 0.59 mpy, up to 280 times less than hydrochloric acid in the same comparison.

The same testing measured 15% more calcium-carbonate capacity than hydrochloric acid.

That pairing matters: more capacity for the deposit and much less attack on the steel beneath it.

Replace the acid workflow as well as the acid

HCR is HMIS 0-0-0, non-fuming, non-hazmat for transport, and requires no product-specific PPE or ventilation for routine use.

The maintenance task may still require lockout, pressure control, confined-space protection, gas management, splash controls, and site wastewater procedures.

The commercial advantage is a simpler chemical entering an already controlled maintenance job.

In a healthcare mechanical room, coordinate the water-side shutdown with the facility's water-management team. Flushing, stagnant branches, treatment residuals, and restart documentation belong in that handoff. The Brevard result puts time into dollars

At a Brevard County School District HVAC system, CLR remained on a 20-year stainless-steel plate and drain for 36 hours without clearing the rust.

DDC Engineering switched to HCR. The crew sprayed it on, waited 30 minutes, and rinsed with a garden hose. No scrubbing was recorded.

That named result shows how faster effective cleaning can change labor, schedule, and return-to-service cost on the right buildup.
