Low-foam degreaser for parts washers and floor scrubbers

MASEST Technical Team · August 3, 2026 · 3 min read

Foam can turn a cleaner into a machine problem

A parts washer or floor scrubber should spend the shift moving soil. When detergent builds a head of foam, the crew stops to manage the tank, chase weak pickup, add defoamer, or rinse again.

That is why the cleaner has to fit both the dirt and the machine. VertKleen CR HD Low Foam brings heavy-duty grease removal to equipment that needs controlled foam.

Start with the soil and the equipment

Oil, hydraulic fluid, carbon, tire marks, and tracked shop grime do not need the same starting setup. Match the wash to the soil load, bath volume, water temperature, cycle, and finish you need.

Start with one repeatable load or one clearly marked floor lane. The result becomes easier to see and easier to repeat.

Run one repeatable cycle

Keep the load, operator, water temperature, cycle time, spray pattern, and finish target consistent. Record where the current cleaner creates foam, extra rinsing, or rework.

Then run CR HD Low Foam through the same job and compare the finished part or lane, not the activity inside the tank.

Why CR HD Low Foam fits machine washing

CR HD Low Foam lifts grease and oily residue while keeping foam down so pumps, scrubbers, recovery tanks, and parts washers can keep moving. Faster rinsing and fewer foam interruptions give the machine more time to clean.

It is a non-butyl alternative to solvent and butyl degreasers, ships non-hazmat, and carries HMIS 0-0-0.

Compare the whole wash, not the concentrate

A low jug price can disappear inside extra stops. Count clean parts per cycle, foam interruptions, rinse water, operator time, rework, bath life, and how quickly the equipment is ready for the next load.

The best cleaner is the one that reaches the finish while letting the machine do its job.

HMIS 0-0-0 fits equipment work

CR HD Low Foam is HMIS 0-0-0, matching every current VertKleen product MASEST offers. That gives maintenance teams one simpler profile for freight, receiving, storage, training, and everyday use.

The product stays focused on heavy soil without adding a high-hazard chemistry profile beside valuable wash equipment.

Prove it on one repeatable load

Choose parts with similar soil or mark one scrubber lane. Photograph the starting condition, run the planned cycle, inspect dry, and record every stop.
