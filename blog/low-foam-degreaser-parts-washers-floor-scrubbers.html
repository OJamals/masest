Foam is downtime inside a machine

A floor scrubber or parts washer should move dirt for the full cycle.

When detergent fills the recovery tank with foam, crews stop for weak pickup, defoamer, extra rinsing, or an early dump. The machine is busy, but the job is not advancing.

CR HD Low Foam keeps the chemistry focused on grease removal.

Match the cleaner to both soil and equipment

EquipmentRecurring soilLow-foam advantage
Parts washerOil, hydraulic fluid, and carbonStable recirculation and easier rinse
Automatic scrubberTire marks, grease, and tracked grimeBetter recovery with fewer foam stops
Recirculating wash systemRepeated oily loadsEasier bath and cycle control

Set the working dilution from the current label, soil load, bath volume, temperature, and required finish.

Give the machine one repeatable cycle

Load comparable parts or mark one floor lane. Keep temperature, pad or nozzle, cycle time, and operator consistent.

Watch the recovery tank as closely as the cleaned surface. Record foam stops, pickup, solution used, rinse water, and parts or square feet completed.

The best result leaves the asset clean and the machine ready for the next load.

Heavy degreasing without butyl or solvent logistics

CR HD Low Foam is a non-butyl alternative for heavy oil and grease work. It is HMIS 0-0-0 and ships non-hazmat.

Routine use adds no product-specific PPE or ventilation requirement.

Crews still use the controls required for moving equipment, oily parts, heated baths, pinch points, wet floors, and recovered waste.

Calculate machine-hour economics

Price the concentrate per completed load or cleaned floor area.

Include tank stops, defoamer, rinse water, operator time, bath changes, rework, and lost machine time. A low-foam formula earns its place by completing more useful cleaning in the same shift.
