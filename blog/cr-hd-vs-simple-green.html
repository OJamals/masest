CR HD vs Simple Green for heavy industrial degreasing

MASEST Technical Team · July 9, 2026 · 2 min read

Routine dirt and industrial grime are different jobs

Simple Green Industrial is a capable general cleaner and degreaser. Its directions cover uses from full strength to 1:10, which can work well for everyday facility cleaning.

Old grease, tracked oil, carbon, and heavy shop soil ask more of a cleaner. On floors, forklifts, drains, and parts, repeat passes quickly become repeat labor.

Why CR HD goes further on heavy soil

VertKleen CR HD is built to wet through greasy film, lift it from the surface, and keep loosened soil from settling back down.

The payoff is a cleaner surface with fewer repeat passes at the right dilution—not a mountain of foam.

Simple Green brings a familiar broad-cleaning profile and EPA Safer Choice recognition.

CR HD brings more capacity for heavy hydrocarbon soil plus an HMIS 0-0-0 rating. The clearest comparison is the same dirty surface, cleaned to the same finish.
