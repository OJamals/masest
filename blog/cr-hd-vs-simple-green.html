Simple Green is familiar. CR HD is built for the harder shift.

Simple Green Industrial is a broad cleaner and degreaser. Its published directions span full strength through 1:10.

CR HD is designed for heavier oil, grease, carbon, and tracked shop soil, with dilution range that lets one concentrate move from equipment to floors and glass.

More active cleaning capacity changes the job

CR HD is a 50% degreaser, compared with 15% active for Simple Green.

That added capacity matters when the current cleaner needs repeated application, long scrubbing, or a nearly neat mix to reach the required finish.

CR HD wets through oily film, releases it from the surface, and helps carry the loosened soil away.

Three Walmart distribution centers made the switch

Walmart distribution centers DC-8851, DC-7023, and DC-6099 replaced Simple Green with CR HD.

The same concentrate served workshops, Crown Forklift and Plug Power equipment, parts, kitchens, floors, drains, windows, and glass.

The three-site result shows CR HD consolidating products under a demanding mixed industrial workload.

The safety and logistics advantage is operational

CR HD is HMIS 0-0-0, ships non-hazmat, and adds no product-specific PPE or ventilation requirement for routine use.

Crews still use protection required by oily soil, pressure equipment, traffic, wastewater, and the task itself.

For purchasing teams, the gain reaches receiving, storage, branch transfers, training, and day-to-day handling.
