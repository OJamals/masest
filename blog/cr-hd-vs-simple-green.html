CR HD vs Simple Green for heavy industrial degreasing

MASEST Technical Team · July 9, 2026 · 2 min read

Start with the soil load

Simple Green Industrial is a broad cleaner and degreaser with use guidance from full strength to 1:10 for many jobs. That range can suit routine facility cleaning.

Heavy petroleum grease, tracked oil, carbon, and old shop soil create a different test. On floors, forklifts, drains, and parts, the question is how many passes reach the endpoint.

CR HD is built for the heavier job

VertKleen CR HD combines high-pH wetting, sequestration, and surfactant lift. Wetting enters the film, surfactants lift soil, and sequestration controls hard-water ions and redeposition.

The aim is not maximum foam. It is controlled contact, soil release, rinse-out, and fewer repeat passes at the right dilution.

Compare the products without caricature

Simple Green says its Industrial Cleaner & Degreaser is EPA Safer Choice recognized and needs no special PPE for intended use. A fair comparison should respect that profile.

CR HD’s case is stronger soil capacity plus an HMIS 0-0-0 profile. Prove the difference on the same surface, soil loading, dilution, dwell, agitation, rinse, and endpoint.

Use the 0-0-0 advantage accurately

Every offered VertKleen product is HMIS 0-0-0. For CR HD, that pairs heavy-duty soil lift with a lower-hazard operating profile.

That does not erase procedure. Keep the current label and SDS with the method, protect the surface, control runoff, and verify the rinse and release endpoint.
