CR HD vs Simple Green

MASEST Technical Team · July 9, 2026 · 1 min read

Industrial soil loading

Heavy hydrocarbon soil on floors, forklifts, drains, parts, and equipment can exhaust a general-purpose cleaner quickly. VertKleen CR HD is built for higher soil loading through high-pH wetting, sequestration, and surfactant lift.

Wet, lift, and rinse

Wetting penetrates greasy film; surfactants surround and lift the soil; sequestration controls hard-water ions and limits redeposition. Match foam, agitation, contact time, and rinse to the process instead of repeating weak passes.

Count handling and work-area controls

Current VertKleen documentation records HMIS 0-0-0, non-hazmat shipping, and no special ventilation or area-clearance requirement for routine use. Eye or skin contact may be mildly irritating, without chemical burns or permanent damage. Keep the current label and SDS with the task method.

Compare the completed task

CR HD lists at $10.61/gal; Simple Green Industrial is listed at $13.20-$36.80/gal in the comparison source. Add dilution, passes, labor, water, downtime, and finished result to calculate cost per completed task.
