VertKleen HCR vs RYDLYME: compare the complete descaling job

MASEST Technical Team · July 9, 2026 · 2 min read

Both products deserve a system-level test

RYDLYME is a recognized biodegradable descaler. Its maker reports capacity of 2 pounds of calcium carbonate per gallon and typical circulation cycles of 2–4 hours.

VertKleen HCR targets the same industrial decision with controlled hydrogen-ion activity, wetting, complexing, and inhibition—plus an HMIS 0-0-0 profile.

HCR changes the material-risk question

Source testing reports HCR at 0.59 mpy on steel—up to 280× less corrosion than hydrochloric acid under the reported comparison. That matters when the asset is worth more than the chemical.

Material fit still depends on the system. Confirm metallurgy, seals, coatings, concentration, temperature, circulation, gas release, and contact time before full-scale work.

HMIS 0-0-0 is part of the cycle

Every offered VertKleen product is HMIS 0-0-0. With HCR, the handling profile sits beside industrial scale and rust removal, rather than being traded against it.

Keep the current label and SDS in the trial file. Follow site controls for isolation, circulation, ventilation, rinse, wastewater, inspection, and restart.

Use field proof as the starting signal

At a Brevard County School District HVAC system, an incumbent CLR attempt ran for 36 hours without derusting a 20-year stainless-steel plate and drain. DDC Engineering then selected HCR; the field report records a 30-minute application, hose rinse, and no scrubbing.

That is not a promised cycle for every system. It shows why a bounded HCR comparison belongs in the decision. Review the full field case.
