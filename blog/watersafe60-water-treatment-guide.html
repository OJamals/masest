One formula can cover more of the water-system calendar

WaterSafe60 supports pH adjustment, scale and corrosion control, pipe cleaning, well drilling, and well rehabilitation.

That range gives water teams one familiar acid-free chemistry for ongoing feed, planned cleaning, and recovery work.

Begin with the system goal

GoalHow WaterSafe60 fits
Adjust pHMetered treatment against a measured starting point
Control scale and corrosionOngoing feed based on water and operating data
Clean pipe or equipmentIsolated circulation or soak
Rehabilitate a wellCalculated treatment followed by purge
Support drillingAddition to the drilling-water program

A defined goal determines the volume, feed method, sample points, and finish.

Make the field details explicit: the injection point, reachable sample taps, flushing route, displaced water, and starting pH, flow, and pressure. The next operator should be able to compare the finished condition with the same readings.

Energy is a major part of a utility's operating budget, so the water team also cares about pumping effort and repeat work. EPA's utility guidance identifies energy as the largest controllable cost in water and wastewater services.

NSF 60 supports potable-water applications

NSF/ANSI/CAN 60 addresses health effects for chemicals used to treat drinking water.

WaterSafe60 carries this certification for potable-water treatment within its certified use.

See the WaterSafe60 certification details/proof#watersafe60-nsf60

For utilities, well contractors, hospitals, campuses, and water-service companies, that gives technical and operations teams a recognized basis for specification.

Measure before adding product

Record system volume, starting pH, hardness, flow, pressure, and visible deposit. Mark the sample locations and the operating target.

For pH adjustment, use a bench titration to establish a starting dose. For cleaning, map the circulation or soak path and where released material will leave the system.

The current WaterSafe60 directions provide application-specific use levels.

Match delivery to the work

Ongoing pH, scale, and corrosion programs need controlled metering and trend data.

Pipe and vessel cleaning work as isolated, planned jobs. Well rehabilitation adds calculated product, contact time, and a purge that continues while pH returns to the normal range.

Drilling use belongs inside the contractor’s water and formation program.

Leave mineral-acid logistics behind

WaterSafe60 is acid-free, water-soluble, mild in odor, HMIS 0-0-0, and non-hazmat for shipping.

Routine use adds no product-specific PPE or ventilation requirement.

Pressure, well access, pumps, electrical equipment, confined areas, discharge, and the treated system still determine job protection and operating controls.

Price treatment by system result

For an ongoing program, trend product use against pH stability, scale condition, corrosion indicators, flow, and maintenance intervals.

For cleaning or rehabilitation, count product, labor, circulation, purge water, and time until the system returns to its target readings.

The economic value is a stable or restored system, not an empty container.
