VertKleen HCR vs CLR PRO MAX for industrial descaling

MASEST Technical Team · July 9, 2026 · 2 min read

CLR PRO MAX is an industrial product

A useful comparison starts with the current product, not an outdated caricature. CLR PRO MAX is sold for heavy industrial descaling, rust, lime, and calcium work.

Its technical data starts heavy-duty applications at 1:1 and lists a 2–3 hour use window. Its current SDS classifies the sold product and use dilution for serious skin and eye hazards.

Review the current CLR PRO MAX product informationhttps://www.clrbrands.com/proline/products/clr-pro/clr-pro-max-industrial-descaler/

HCR makes a different operating case

VertKleen HCR uses controlled hydrogen-ion activity to consume carbonate scale. Wetting, complexing, and inhibition support deposit release and rinse-out.

Every offered VertKleen product is HMIS 0-0-0. HCR pairs industrial scale and rust removal with that lower-hazard operating profile.

HCR protects more of the asset value

Source testing reports HCR at 0.59 mpy on steel—up to 280× less corrosion than hydrochloric acid under the reported comparison.

The source record also reports 15% more calcium-carbonate capacity than hydrochloric acid. Those figures support an equipment-conscious trial on real deposit.

Read the field result carefully

At a Brevard County School District HVAC system, CLR was used for 36 hours without derusting a 20-year stainless-steel plate and drain. DDC Engineering then selected HCR; the field report records a 30-minute application, garden-hose rinse, and no scrubbing.

The source identifies CLR, not today’s CLR PRO MAX formula. This is field evidence for HCR on that severe job—not a direct test of the current competitor product. Read the full case record.
