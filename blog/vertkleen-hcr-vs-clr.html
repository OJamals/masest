VertKleen HCR vs CLR PRO MAX for industrial descaling

MASEST Technical Team · July 9, 2026 · 2 min read

Same job, very different operating story

CLR PRO MAX is sold for heavy industrial scale, rust, lime, and calcium. Its technical data starts heavy-duty work at 1:1 and lists a 2–3 hour use window.

Its current SDS also lists serious skin and eye hazards for the sold product and use dilution.

See CLR PRO MAX product informationhttps://www.clrbrands.com/proline/products/clr-pro/clr-pro-max-industrial-descaler/

Why HCR changes the equation

VertKleen HCR uses controlled mineral-removal chemistry to break down carbonate scale and rust.

Wetting and corrosion-control ingredients help the buildup release and rinse away.

HCR is also HMIS 0-0-0, giving crews industrial descaling power with a much simpler handling profile.

Protect more of the equipment value

Source testing measured HCR at 0.59 mpy on steel—up to 280× less corrosion than hydrochloric acid in the same comparison.

The same testing found 15% more calcium-carbonate capacity than hydrochloric acid.

That means more scale-removal capacity with far less attack on the steel underneath it.

See the 36-hour versus 30-minute result

At a Brevard County School District HVAC system, CLR worked for 36 hours without clearing rust from a 20-year stainless-steel plate and drain.

DDC Engineering switched to HCR. The crew sprayed it on, waited 30 minutes, and rinsed with a garden hose—no scrubbing recorded.
