VertKleen HCR vs CLR

MASEST Technical Team · July 9, 2026 · 1 min read

Industrial deposit load changes the comparison

CLR is commonly used for light-duty rust and calcium cleaning. VertKleen HCR is positioned for larger deposit loads, circulation, concentrated use, and industrial equipment where completed-system cost matters more than one bottle.

Carbonate chemistry drives the cycle

HCR uses controlled hydrogen-ion activity to consume carbonate scale, with wetting, complexing, and inhibition supporting release and rinse-out. Compare concentration, circulation time, equipment scale, fumes, material condition, rinse, and crew experience.

Include the operating profile

Current VertKleen documentation records HMIS 0-0-0, non-hazmat shipping, and no special ventilation or area-clearance requirement for routine use. Eye or skin contact may be mildly irritating, without chemical burns or permanent damage. Follow the current product label and SDS.
