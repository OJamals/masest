VertKlean HCR vs CLR

MASEST Technical Team · July 9, 2026 · 1 min read

Industrial deposit load changes the comparison

CLR is commonly used for light-duty rust and calcium cleaning. VertKlean HCR is positioned for larger deposit loads, circulation, concentrated use, and industrial equipment where completed-system cost matters more than one bottle.

Carbonate chemistry drives the cycle

HCR uses controlled hydrogen-ion activity to consume carbonate scale, with wetting, complexing, and inhibition supporting release and rinse-out. Compare concentration, circulation time, equipment scale, fumes, material condition, rinse, and crew experience.

Price the completed job

HCR lists at $21.63/gal; CLR PRO MAX is listed at $29.09-$32.73/gal in the comparison source. Add use rate, labor, shutdown time, rinse water, and finished result before choosing the lower-cost path.
