The cheapest cleaner is the one that finishes the job

A low unit price stops looking economical when crews need extra product, more scrubbing, and a second pass.

Motor pools and public-works shops need fast results across grease, road film, carbon, ordinary grime, rust, and mineral scale.

A useful standard does not force one cleaner onto every asset. It gives buyers a small, repeatable product set tied to the work.

The Military / Government guide shows that asset-first approach.

Make every line item explain its job

Use CR HD for fleet, motor-pool, and equipment degreasing.

Use VertKleen Neutral for compatible everyday shop and facility surfaces.

Specify HCR or Descaler for controlled scale removal on compatible heat-transfer and water-side assets.

Keep the product link, working dilution, pack size, and restock point with the bay's work instruction. A motor-pool supervisor should be able to reorder for road film, oily undercarriages, and parts cleaning without rebuilding the same request for every department.

GSA's fleet guidance emphasizes scheduled preventive maintenance and vehicle history. Tie the cleaning task to the unit number and next dispatch so a clean vehicle also arrives ready for its inspection or service appointment.

Before cleaning, identify the asset, deposit, wash-water path, access limits, and finished condition.

For vehicles, that may mean service points and undercarriage areas ready for inspection.

For an exchanger, it means the defined water-side surface ready for the next maintenance step.

The economic case is direct: the matched product works faster, reduces manual scrubbing and repeat passes, and lets one focused order support recurring work across fleet and facilities.

Shipping is nonhazardous, which also removes the hazmat surcharge from the purchase.

Make restocking repeatable across shops

VertKleen products are effective, non-toxic, noncorrosive, nonflammable, and HMIS 0-0-0.

Normal use requires no chemical-specific PPE or ventilation, making routine handling simpler across authorized maintenance teams.

Agency and installation requirements still govern vehicle movement, security, utility isolation, environmental controls, task PPE, and wash-water recovery.

Keep those instructions with the local work order.
