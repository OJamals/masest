How to test a safer industrial cleaner before you switch

MASEST Technical Team · July 26, 2026 · 2 min read

A cleaner switch should be easy to judge

Good products often get bad trials: the wrong soil, a weak dilution, different tools, or no shared idea of what clean should look like.

A simple side-by-side test fixes that. It shows whether VertKleen finishes the same job better, faster, or with less effort.

Match the cleaner to the mess

Start with the surface and what is stuck to it.

Scale and rust need mineral removal. Grease, oil, protein, and organic film need wetting and soil lift. Mixed buildup may work best as a two-step clean.

Choose the VertKleen product around the real job, not the product name that sounds closest.

Run the cleaners side by side

Use equal areas or matching parts. Keep the soil, tools, dwell time, rinse, and finish target as close as the job allows.

This makes the result easy for operators, purchasing, and management to see together.

Keep the first test small

Start on a test patch, removable part, or one clearly marked area.

Protect nearby materials and plan where the rinse water will go. Then clean, rinse, inspect, and put the area back to work.

A small test keeps the decision quick and gives the team room to adjust the method before expanding.

Look beyond gallon price

Every VertKleen product MASEST offers is HMIS 0-0-0.

That can make receiving, storage, setup, crew handling, and cleanup easier than a conventional acid, caustic, or solvent process.

Count those gains beside product use, passes, water, labor, finish quality, and downtime.

Turn one good result into the new method

Save the product, dilution, contact time, tools, water, labor, photos, and total job cost.

Those notes become the crew's starting method, training guide, and purchasing case for the next area.

No guesswork. No decision based on jug price alone.
