The fastest credible path to replacing a harsh chemical

MASEST Technical Team · July 26, 2026 · 1 min read

Diagnose the soil before choosing chemistry

Start with the asset, deposit or soil, material, temperature, current chemical, operating window, and desired result. Mineral scale points toward controlled mineral removal; grease and organic film point toward wetting, sequestration, and soil lift.

Build a side-by-side trial

  1. Baseline. Record current dose, contact time, passes, rinse water, crew time, and finished result.
  2. Match. Choose the exact VertKleen product whose mechanism fits the soil and surface.
  3. Compare. Hold the asset, soil load, method, and acceptance endpoint constant.
  4. Observe. Capture cleaning result, material fit, odor, handling, rinse demand, and return-to-service time.
  5. Verify handling. Confirm the current HMIS, shipping classification, work-area controls, and contact guidance for the exact SKU and concentration.

Make the result reusable

Record product, concentration, method, crew time, rinse volume, before/after endpoint, and completed-task cost. That turns one trial into a repeatable replacement method.
