Cooling-tower cleaning belongs inside the water-management plan

MASEST Technical Team · July 26, 2026 · 1 min read

Give every chemistry one clear role

A coordinated tower program separates recurring control from periodic cleaning. WaterSafe60 manages scale and corrosion pressure; Purgo works on organic residue and microbial burden; HCR or Descaler removes established carbonate scale, rust, and mineral deposits during a cleaning window.

Build the maintenance window around the system

Record tower duty, system volume, metallurgy, deposit, current treatment, circulation path, shutdown window, rinse volume, and operating endpoint. Those inputs set product, concentration, contact time, and completed-system cost.

Match the mechanism to the deposit

Use controlled mineral-removal chemistry for carbonate scale and corrosion deposits. Use soil lift for oily or organic film. Use bio-active chemistry for persistent organic load. The program becomes easier to run when one product is not asked to solve every problem.

Make the result reusable

Record dose, circulation time, crew time, rinse water, deposit change, and return-to-service time. Compare those values with the current program, not gallon price alone.
