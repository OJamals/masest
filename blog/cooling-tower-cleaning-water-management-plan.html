A better way to plan cooling-tower cleaning

MASEST Technical Team · July 26, 2026 · 2 min read

Cleaning works best as part of the plan

A cooling tower changes every day. Heat, evaporation, makeup water, airborne debris, and biological growth all shape what builds up inside it.

The best programs pair ongoing treatment with well-timed cleaning, so crews remove old buildup and keep it from coming back quickly.

Give every product one clear job

WaterSafe60 helps control scale and corrosion over time. Purgo helps control organic buildup and odor. HCR or Descaler removes mineral scale already on the system.

NeedVertKleen productBest time to use it
Scale and corrosion between cleaningsWaterSafe60Ongoing water treatment
Organic buildup and odorPurgoOngoing treatment
Existing mineral buildupHCR or DescalerPlanned cleaning window
Oily filmCR or CR HDWhen oily film appears

Look at the buildup before choosing a cleaner

Hard mineral scale calls for HCR or Descaler. Oily film points to CR or CR HD. Purgo helps with organic buildup and odor.

A quick inspection of the fill, basin, piping, and heat-transfer surfaces points the team toward the right product before shutdown day.

Plan the restart before the shutdown

Work backward from the time the tower must be running again. Include access, circulation, rinsing, water disposal, inspection, refill, and treatment restart.

That plan exposes the real schedule and keeps a small cleaning delay from becoming a long operating delay.

HMIS 0-0-0 makes the workday easier

Every VertKleen product MASEST offers is HMIS 0-0-0.

For tower teams, that can mean easier freight, storage, work-area planning, and crew coordination without giving up industrial cleaning power.

Give each product one clear job and crews get a cleaning plan that is easier to run, repeat, and improve.

Measure what the system gets back

Track deposit removal, water use, crew time, surface condition, and total shutdown time.

The goal is not an empty drum. It is a cleaner tower, a smoother restart, and a program the team can repeat next time.

Keep the lesson for the next shutdown

Save the photos, cleaning notes, restart readings, and next inspection date with the water-management plan.

That turns one maintenance event into a better starting point for the next one.
