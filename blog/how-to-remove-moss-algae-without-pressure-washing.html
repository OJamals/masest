How to remove moss and algae without pressure washing

MASEST Technical Team · August 3, 2026 · 3 min read

Moss and algae reward the dampest corners

Green staining settles into shaded walkways, pavers, brick, siding, concrete, and roof surfaces. Blasting the color away can turn a cleaning job into a surface-repair job.

VertKleen LAM3 gives property teams a low-pressure route built around coverage and time.

Start with the stain, not the pressure setting

First identify what is growing and where it holds moisture. LAM3 targets lichen, algae, moss, mold, and mildew stains.

It works across pavers, wood, stucco, siding, metal, aluminum, painted surfaces, concrete, brick, glass, tile, and roofing materials.

Pick a small test area that matches the full surface. Let it dry before judging the color and finish.

Mix for heavy or light staining

For heavy lichen, algae, moss, mold, or mildew staining, mix one part LAM3 with five parts fresh water. For lighter staining, use one part product with ten parts water.

Even coverage matters more than force. Apply with a low-pressure sprayer or brush, then let the treatment work.

Let chemistry replace unnecessary pressure

LAM3 contains no acid, caustic, solvent, bleach, quat, or peroxide. It is pH neutral and HMIS 0-0-0.

For heavily soiled areas, brush and rinse after application, then reapply if needed. Pressure washing does not have to be the default.

Results keep developing after the crew leaves

Visible change can start within a week or two. Spray-and-leave jobs may take up to a month to reach their maximum result.

That longer window changes how the work should be judged. Take the same photo angle on day one, then check the area again over the next several weeks.

Reapply when staining returns or every six months as needed.

One route can cover mixed exterior materials

Commercial properties rarely offer one perfect test slab. A single route may cross concrete, brick, painted trim, siding, glass, and pavers.

LAM3 gives crews one starting method across those materials: test, mix, apply evenly, let time work, then brush or rinse only where the surface needs it.
