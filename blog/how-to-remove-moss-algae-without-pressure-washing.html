How to remove moss and algae without pressure washing

A low-pressure VertKleen LAM3 plan for moss, algae, lichen, mold, and mildew stains on concrete, brick, siding, pavers, and more.

Let time do the work that pressure often does badly

Moss, algae, lichen, mold, and mildew settle into shaded concrete, brick, pavers, siding, wood, roofs, and painted surfaces.

High pressure may remove the color quickly while damaging joints, coatings, fibers, or the surface itself. LAM3 uses even coverage and working time instead.

Match the mix to the staining

For heavy organic staining, the current LAM3 directions call for one part product to five parts fresh water.

For lighter staining, use one part product to ten parts water.

Apply with a low-pressure sprayer or brush. Saturate the growth evenly and let the treatment remain on the surface.

Expect the result to keep developing

Visible improvement can begin within one or two weeks. A spray-and-leave treatment may take up to a month to reach its maximum result.

Photograph the same view on day one and at planned intervals. The finished-area value appears over time, not in a rushed same-minute reveal.

For heavily soiled areas, brush and rinse after application, then reapply as directed. Repeat when staining returns or at the labeled maintenance interval.

A neutral formula changes where crews can work

LAM3 contains no acid, caustic, solvent, bleach, quat, or peroxide. It is pH neutral, HMIS 0-0-0, zero-VOC, mild in odor, and ships non-hazmat.

Routine use adds no product-specific PPE or ventilation requirement.

Roof access, fall protection, spray equipment, weather, biological soil, landscaping, and runoff still determine the protection for the job.

One product can cross a mixed property

LAM3 is designed for pavers, wood, stucco, siding, metal, aluminum, painted surfaces, concrete, brick, glass, tile, and roofing materials.

Test a small area and inspect it dry, especially on aged coatings or porous stone. Then keep application rate and photo angle consistent across the property.
