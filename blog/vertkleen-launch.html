Start with the cleaning problem

VertKleen is a 15-product system, not one universal jug. The fastest path to a good trial is to name the soil, surface, process, and endpoint before choosing chemistry.

Every product MASEST offers is HMIS 0-0-0. That gives the whole line one lower-hazard baseline while each formula keeps a specific job.

HCR, HVAC HCR, and Descaler

Use the mineral-removal group for carbonate scale, rust, and deposits in equipment, piping, HVAC, and water systems. Match the product to metallurgy, volume, deposit, circulation, and shutdown plan.

Source testing reports HCR at 0.59 mpy on steel, up to 280× less corrosion than hydrochloric acid, with 15% more calcium-carbonate capacity than HCl.

CR, HVAC CR, and the CR HD family

CR and HVAC CR target organic soil through wetting, alkalinity, and sequestration. CR HD adds capacity for heavy hydrocarbon loading. Low Foam fits processes where foam control matters.

Use soil loading, dilution, passes, water, labor, and surface condition to choose among them. The strongest concentrate is not automatically the best process.

Neutral and MultiWash

Neutral supports surface-conscious general cleaning. MultiWash covers mixed facility work where one practical maintenance cleaner can reduce product switching.

Confirm the substrate, soil, finish, application method, rinse, and release endpoint before standardizing either product.

Purgo and LAM3

Purgo supports organic-load and odor programs. LAM3 extends wetting and dwell across moss, algae, mildew, lichen, and visible exterior staining.

Their value appears over the planned treatment interval. Record coverage, weather, visible change, labor, runoff, adjacent materials, and time to endpoint.

AlumiBrite, Torque, SAR, and WaterSafe60

AlumiBrite targets aluminum cleaning and brightening. Torque supports wash-and-finish care. SAR handles matched rust-and-scale work. WaterSafe60 supports recurring scale and corrosion control.

These are specialist tools. Use the current product page, label, SDS, and a bounded trial to confirm the fit.

One safety profile, 50+ countries

Every offered VertKleen product is HMIS 0-0-0, and the platform works across 50+ countries. That combination supports simpler portfolio, freight, receiving, storage, and training conversations.

Local rules and product-specific methods still apply. Keep the exact label, SDS, dilution, wastewater path, and site procedure connected to the task.

Turn the match into a controlled trial

Name the asset, substrate, soil, current chemical, temperature, method, operating window, and acceptance endpoint. Isolate a coupon, part, or bounded area when possible.

Record concentration, contact time, agitation, passes, rinse water, crew time, wastewater, surface condition, downtime, and completed-task cost.

Use the product catalog, decision guides, and field results to prepare the switch.
