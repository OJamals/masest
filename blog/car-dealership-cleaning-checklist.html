A dealership is one customer journey with five cleaning zones

The service drive is filling up, a used car is waiting on recon, and the next delivery still needs clean glass and rocker panels. Cleaning has to support those handoffs while vehicles keep arriving.

Give the wash team, detail bay, and service writer the same finish standard: no greasy transfer, no glass streaks, and a vehicle ready for its next repair order or customer delivery. NADA's fixed-operations guidance tracks shop utilization and repair-order flow—the same rhythm a useful cleaning plan should support.

A useful cleaning plan protects that journey while keeping technicians, detailers, and sales staff moving.

These products are HMIS 0-0-0, ship non-hazmat, and have no product-specific PPE or ventilation requirement for routine use. Bay, vehicle, machine, and slip controls remain part of the job.

Stop shop grime at the service-drive boundary

Close completed bays first. Dry-pick debris, give oily areas enough CR HD working time, agitate where needed, recover the dirty solution, and inspect edges before reopening the lane.

Move next to mats, doors, counters, and the path to the waiting area. This order keeps oil and tire soil from following the mop into customer space.

Use Neutral when pH 7.5 is the better fit for seals, coatings, painted equipment, or frequent cleaning.

Make vehicle prep a two-product decision

Torque combines vehicle washing with finish care, reducing the need to manage a separate wash and wax sequence.

Use AlumiBrite only for oxidized aluminum that needs restoration. Its HF- and HCl-free formula avoids the acid brightener workflow while delivering a cleaner, brighter metal finish.

The Durango result shows the dilution range

A Durango that had gone several weeks without washing was cleaned with CR HD at about 1:100.

The before-and-after photos showed a clean finish with no damage to the clear coat or accessories.

That result gives dealership operators a practical lesson: heavy-duty concentrate can still serve finish-sensitive work when the dilution matches the soil.

Measure the close by ready zones

Track when each service lane, showroom section, and vehicle becomes ready for use. Add product consumed, repeat passes, water, and rework.

This exposes the true cost per completed lane or prepared vehicle and makes recurring orders easier to forecast.

Order CR HD, Torque, or AlumiBrite directly, or review fleet and car-wash applications.
