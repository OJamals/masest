HMIS 0-0-0: what it means—and what it changes

MASEST Technical Team · July 1, 2026 · 2 min read

Every offered VertKleen product is 0-0-0

MASEST now offers 15 VertKleen products, and every one is HMIS 0-0-0. The former non-0-0-0 product is no longer offered.

That gives the current line one clear operating story: cleaning performance without the hazard profile that often surrounds industrial acid, caustic, bleach, and solvent work.

Read the three digits in order

HMIS uses three numeric fields from 0 to 4. A zero is the lowest rating in its field. The rating belongs to the exact product and current documentation.

PositionHMIS fieldVertKleen rating
FirstHealth0
SecondFlammability0
ThirdPhysical hazard0

Zero does not mean “ignore the label”

HMIS 0-0-0 does not turn an industrial product into drinking water. It does not replace the label, SDS, dilution, surface check, wastewater plan, or site SOP.

Standard work hygiene still applies. Avoid unnecessary contact, use the product for its intended task, and follow current first-aid and handling instructions.

The rating changes more than PPE

A chemical’s operating burden begins before application and continues after cleaning. Freight, receiving, storage, training, area coordination, spill planning, rinse, cleanup, and release all consume time.

VertKleen’s 0-0-0 profile can simplify those steps while keeping the cleaning result at the center of the decision.

Workflow pointQuestion to compare

One line is easier to standardize

A universal 0-0-0 profile reduces exceptions across a multi-product program. Teams can match chemistry to soil without reintroducing a high-hazard outlier from the same offered line.

That helps purchasing, safety, maintenance, and operations discuss the same portfolio with a consistent baseline. The task method stays product-specific.

It supports global movement

VertKleen works across 50+ countries. The 0-0-0 profile supports a cleaner receiving and handling story for distributors, facilities, contractors, and international programs.

Local transport, import, workplace, and discharge rules still apply. Confirm the current documents and destination requirements for each shipment.

Put handling beside performance

Do not choose a cleaner on HMIS alone. Compare deposit or soil removal, material condition, dose, passes, water, labor, downtime, wastewater, and completed-task cost.

Then place the 0-0-0 operating profile beside those results. That is where lower hazard becomes a measurable business advantage instead of a label footnote.
