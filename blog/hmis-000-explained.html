HMIS 0-0-0: why it matters on the job

MASEST Team · July 1, 2026 · 1 min read

HMIS in plain English

HMIS uses three numbers to describe health, flammability, and physical hazard. Each number runs from 0, the lowest, to 4, the highest.

VertKleen products are rated HMIS 0-0-0. That gives crews a simple, consistent starting point for receiving, storage, training, and everyday handling.

Every VertKleen product MASEST offers is rated HMIS 0-0-0.

What the three numbers mean

NumberCoversVertKleen rating
FirstHealth0
SecondFlammability0
ThirdPhysical hazard0

HMIS is one useful part of the product picture. Use it with the label, SDS, a small surface check, and your workplace safety rules.

Check the product before use

Read the latest label and SDS for the VertKleen product and package you have. Follow its mixing, handling, storage, and rinse directions.

If the cleaner will touch a new finish, coating, seal, or material, try a small hidden area first. Make sure the dry result looks right before cleaning the whole job.

Why customers care

A consistent HMIS 0-0-0 line can make training easier, simplify the chemical cabinet, and give customers a clearer alternative to many harsh conventional cleaners.

The cleaning result still matters most. Compare VertKleen with your current cleaner on the same mess, surface, area, tools, working time, and finish target.
