Less handling overhead. More time cleaning.

Industrial cleaning gets expensive when the chemical adds work: special handling, ventilation setup, freight surcharges, and time waiting for an area to clear.

VertKleen combines HMIS 0-0-0 with non-toxic, noncorrosive, nonflammable formulations built for demanding cleaning jobs. The benefit reaches the whole workday, from receiving a shipment to putting equipment back to work.

What the three zeros mean

HMIS rates health, flammability, and physical hazard from 0 to 4. Zero is the lowest rating in each category. Every VertKleen product MASEST offers carries HMIS 0-0-0.

RatingCategoryWhat it tells your team
0HealthThe lowest HMIS health-hazard rating
0FlammabilityThe lowest HMIS flammability rating
0Physical hazardThe lowest HMIS physical-hazard rating

The product line also has practical handling and shipping advantages beyond the rating itself.

Fit routine cleaning into the workday

For routine use, VertKleen products require no product-specific PPE or special ventilation. Crews can handle everyday cleaning without adding a chemical-fume clearance period or a ventilation shutdown to the schedule.

That matters for a workshop wiping down equipment, a property team cleaning between service calls, or a warehouse removing tracked grime before the next shift.

The job still determines its own controls. Pressure washing, hot equipment, traffic, contaminated soil, and work at height may require protection or isolation. Equipment rinsing and required sanitation also remain part of the job.

VertKleen simplifies the chemical side of that work. Follow the selected product's directions for dilution, application, and rinsing.

Keep the cleaning power

Avoid hazmat shipping surcharges

VertKleen products ship as non-hazmat, without a hazmat shipping surcharge. Ordinary shipping costs still depend on the package, quantity, and destination.

For a buyer supplying several sites, that makes receiving, storage, replenishment, and branch-to-branch planning more straightforward. A consistent product family also makes it easier to train crews and reduce overlapping chemicals on the shelf.
