HMIS 0-0-0: why it matters on the job

MASEST Technical Team · July 1, 2026 · 2 min read

The entire VertKleen line is HMIS 0-0-0

MASEST offers 15 VertKleen products, and every one is HMIS 0-0-0.

That gives customers one clear promise across the line: serious industrial cleaning without the hazard profile common to acids, caustics, bleach, and solvents.

What the three numbers mean

HMIS rates health, flammability, and physical hazard from 0 to 4. Zero is the lowest rating in each category.

PositionHMIS categoryVertKleen rating
FirstHealth0
SecondFlammability0
ThirdPhysical hazard0

What 0-0-0 feels like on the job

Crews still use each product as directed, but the surrounding work can be much simpler.

There is less high-hazard complexity to carry through receiving, storage, training, setup, cleanup, and day-to-day handling.

That matters when a facility cleans often, stores several products, or has many people touching the process.

The advantage starts before cleaning

A chemical affects the job long before it touches the surface. It also affects what happens after the cleaning is finished.

One profile is easier to standardize

A universal HMIS 0-0-0 profile removes the high-hazard outlier from a multi-product cleaning program.

Purchasing, safety, maintenance, and operations can work from the same simple baseline while teams choose the right formula for each job.

Pair easier handling with better cleaning

HMIS is one part of a better chemical decision. The finished result still matters most.
