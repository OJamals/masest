MASEST Team · August 3, 2026 · 3 min read

One cleaner replaced Simple Green across three sites

VertKleen CR HD has been used at Walmart distribution centers DC-8851, DC-7023, and DC-6099.

CR HD became the Simple Green replacement for workshops, forklifts, parts, kitchens, floors, and glass.

That range is the story: one heavy-duty cleaner serving very different parts of a busy distribution center.

More cleaning power for a mixed workload

Distribution centers move from oily equipment to tracked floor dirt, greasy break areas, dirty parts, and streaked glass in the same shift.

CR HD gives crews one concentrate they can tune to each job instead of reaching for a different cleaner every time the surface changes.

CR HD is a 50% degreaser, compared with 15% active for Simple Green.

That extra cleaning capacity gives teams more room to tune dilution while keeping the dirt lift needed for hard jobs.

Put it on the jobs that consume the most time

Start with a dirty floor bay, one forklift, a repeat parts-cleaning job, or a grease-heavy kitchen surface.

Run both cleaners at job-ready dilution and compare the things the crew notices: passes, product used, rinse water, finish, and time back to service.
