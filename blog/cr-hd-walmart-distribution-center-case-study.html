Inside a three-site Walmart distribution-center switch to CR HD

MASEST Technical Team · August 3, 2026 · 4 min read

This is an adoption record—not a controlled shootout

MASEST's source field document records VertKleen CR HD being supplied to three Walmart distribution centers: DC-8851, DC-7023, and DC-6099. It describes CR HD as the Simple Green replacement for heavy facility cleaning across workshops, forklifts, parts, kitchens, floors, and glass.

That is useful operating evidence, but it is not a laboratory head-to-head. The source does not record task-level dilution, soil loading, passes, labor hours, rinse water, or a CR HD savings total. A buyer should treat the three-site adoption as a reason to test—not as a universal result promise.

The same Walmart program brochure reports more than $10,000 per year saved at DC-8851—but that figure belongs to a separate VertKleen Descaler plumbing program. It is not a CR HD savings claim.

The operating environment explains the appeal

Hero image context: this is a generated, unbranded warehouse trial illustration. It does not depict a Walmart facility or a documented cleaning result.

Distribution centers do not have one generic degreasing job. Forklift film, tracked floor soil, parts residue, kitchen grease, and glass streaking create different endpoints. A cleaner that spans those zones can reduce product count, but only when each use has a defined dilution, method, rinse, and acceptance check.

The source describes CR HD as 50% degreaser and Simple Green as 15% active. That comparison supports a stronger-concentrate discussion; concentration alone does not prove lower completed-task cost. Soil capacity, diluted use, passes, labor, water, and rework decide the job.

Build the switch around completed-task cost

A fair switch trial uses matched areas and one visible endpoint. Do not compare a weak dilution on one side with a stronger dose, longer dwell, or extra agitation on the other.

HMIS 0-0-0 belongs in the warehouse decision

Every currently offered VertKleen product is HMIS 0-0-0, including CR HD. That gives a heavy-degreasing program a lower-hazard profile across freight, receiving, storage, training, and use areas.

HMIS 0-0-0 does not remove procedure. Keep the current label and SDS with the method. Confirm surface and equipment fit, protect adjacent materials, control runoff, follow wastewater requirements, and retain the site's normal PPE and release decisions.

Source field image: VertKleen CR HD container and a prepared sample. Always follow the current product label for task-specific dilution.

Run one bounded trial before changing the standard

Choose one recurring, measurable task: a defined floor bay, one forklift exterior, a parts family, or a kitchen surface. Photograph the same view, document the existing cleaner and method, then run matched CR HD conditions against the same clean endpoint.

Record concentration, product volume, contact time, agitation, passes, rinse water, labor, surface condition, and return-to-service. Expand only after the result is repeatable.
