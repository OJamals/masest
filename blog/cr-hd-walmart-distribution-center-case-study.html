Three Walmart distribution centers replaced one familiar cleaner

Walmart DC-8851, DC-7023, and DC-6099 switched from Simple Green to VertKleen CR HD.

The decision covered workshops, forklifts, parts, kitchens, floors, drains, windows, and glass. One concentrate crossed more of the facility without giving up heavy-duty cleaning.

The real advantage was range

Distribution centers move from hydraulic film and forklift grime to food-area grease and streak-sensitive glass in one shift.

CR HD’s dilution range let each crew tune the same concentrate to the soil instead of stocking a separate product for every room.

Work areaWhat CR HD handled
Workshops and partsOil, grease, and shop soil
Crown Forklift and Plug Power equipmentOily equipment film
Warehouse floorsTire marks and tracked grime
Kitchens and drainsGrease and organic soil
Windows and glassLight soil at task-matched dilution

CR HD is a 50% degreaser, compared with 15% active for Simple Green. The extra cleaning capacity created room to dilute for lighter jobs and stay strong on harder ones.

Product consolidation has a balance-sheet effect

Fewer cleaners mean fewer purchase lines, storage positions, secondary bottles, and opportunities to use the wrong chemistry.

A distribution network can also forecast one concentrate against completed floors, cleaned equipment, and branch consumption instead of tracking a shelf of overlapping products.

VertKleen CR HD concentrate and a prepared field sample.

Low foam keeps equipment in the plan

For floor scrubbers and recirculating equipment, CR HD Low Foam keeps the recovery system focused on dirty solution instead of detergent foam.

That matters because tank stops, poor pickup, defoamer, and extra rinsing are labor costs even when they never appear on the chemical invoice.

HMIS 0-0-0 simplified the switch

CR HD is HMIS 0-0-0, ships non-hazmat, and has no product-specific PPE or ventilation requirement for routine use.

Site controls for vehicles, oily residues, slips, wash equipment, and wastewater remain part of the operating method.

The product profile supports simpler receiving, storage, and training across multiple buildings.

What another facility should price

Use the recurring jobs that consume the most labor: one scrubber lane, one forklift type, one parts load, and one grease-heavy surface.

For each, calculate solution used, passes, water, equipment time, rework, and time back to service. Add the inventory and training savings from consolidation.

That is how the Walmart result becomes a purchasing model rather than a one-day demonstration.
