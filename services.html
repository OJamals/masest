Straight answers from the team that scopes the work, before you commit a PO or a site visit.

How are service line items scoped?

MASEST confirms sample type, site access, deliverables, schedule, and final price before dispatch or lab work starts.

Are the published prices final?

They are planning prices. The quote confirms final pricing against sample counts, site conditions, and travel; you approve the confirmed number before any work is billed.

How fast do results come back?

Most water and biological analyses return within five to ten business days of sample receipt. Rush handling is quoted per job when the operating deadline needs it.

Can service work bundle with a chemical switch?

Yes — testing, water-management planning, and field services are routinely scoped alongside a VertKleen replacement so one quote covers the chemistry, field method, and measured result.
