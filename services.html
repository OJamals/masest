Know what happens before work starts.

Straight answers on price, timing, and what the MASEST team needs from you.

What happens after I choose a service?

MASEST confirms the sample, site access, result you need, schedule, and final price before lab or field work starts.

Are the published prices final?

They are starting prices. Your quote reflects the number of samples, location, and travel, and you approve it before work begins.

How fast do results come back?

Most water and biological results arrive within five to ten business days after the sample reaches the lab. Rush work is available when timing is tight.

Can service work bundle with a chemical switch?

Yes. MASEST can put the product, test, field work, and result into one plan and one quote.
