Keep the comparison fair

  1. Pick one job. Use the same kind of equipment, buildup, crew, tools, and finished result for both products.
  2. Write down what happens. Track the mix, working time, rinses, crew time, and downtime.
  3. Look at the finished result. Compare remaining rust and scale, flow, surface condition, and any repeat work.
  4. Count the whole job. Add product, labor, water, equipment time, repeat cleaning, downtime, and cleanup.
