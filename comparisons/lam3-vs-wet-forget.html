Exterior programs should be judged by finished area, not concentrate price. Keep substrate, stain, weather, application method, dwell, runoff control, and inspection interval consistent.

Hold the job constant

  1. Document the baseline. Record asset, substrate, soil or deposit, current product, concentration, temperature, contact time, agitation, passes, rinse water, crew time, and shutdown.
  2. Define the endpoint. Agree on visible residue, flow or heat-transfer recovery, rinse condition, surface condition, and return-to-service requirement before either product is applied.
  3. Test equal areas or circuits. Use the current label, the exact VertKleen product directions, the same operating window, and the same inspection method.
  4. Price the completed result. Add chemistry, labor, water, wastewater handling, equipment time, repeat passes, and production interruption.
