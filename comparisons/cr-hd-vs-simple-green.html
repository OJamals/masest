Keep the comparison fair

  1. Take a before photo. Note the surface, mess, cleaner, mix, tools, time, water, and downtime you use today.
  2. Agree on a good result. Decide what clean looks like before either product touches the surface.
  3. Clean equal areas. Give both products the same crew, tools, area, working time, and final check.
  4. Count the whole job. Compare product, labor, water, repeat passes, downtime, and the finished result.
