How it builds

Buildup never takes a day off.

Minerals crystallize into scale. Steel oxidizes into rust. Grease films harden. Biofilm spreads into the warm, wet layer where Legionella lives. Every layer steals heat transfer, capacity, and the time of whoever gets sent in to clean it.
