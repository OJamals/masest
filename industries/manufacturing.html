On site

How the work is run on site.

Set the job up the way the crew will actually run it, then check the finished result against the same marks every time.

Dilution
Use the current label/TDS range and establish the weakest effective starting point through a material coupon and grime-loading trial.
What to log
Record bath/surface temperature, concentration, working time, spray/brush/flow scrubbing, rinse quality, dry time, and bath-loading limits.
Site controls
LOTO and de-inventory equipment, verify coatings/seals/metallurgy, protect controls and bearings, and segregate incompatible waste streams.
Finished-result check
Water-break or white-wipe acceptance where appropriate, specified surface energy/cleanliness, restored flow or pressure drop, and no corrosion/finish change.
Surfaces
Carbon and stainless steel, aluminum, copper, engineered polymers, elastomers, paint, powder coat, and conversion coatings
Wash water
Profile oil, metals, pH, and solids; use oil-water separation or hazardous/industrial waste routes defined by the plant.
