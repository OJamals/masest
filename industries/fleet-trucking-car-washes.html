On site

How the work is run on site.

Set the job up the way the crew will actually run it, then check the finished result against the same marks every time.

Dilution
Stay within the current wash/degreaser label range and establish a separate tested ratio for aluminum brightening; start with the weakest effective solution.
What to log
Record water hardness, solution and panel temperature, working time, brush or pressure scrubbing, rinse volume, and drying conditions.
Site controls
Keep chemistry off hot panels, brakes, sensors, polished finishes, and unapproved aluminum; follow fleet OEM and wash-equipment limits.
Finished-result check
No road or oily film, no streaking after dry, gloss/finish acceptance, clean wheel test area, and no oily sheen in recovered water.
Surfaces
Clear coat, painted steel, aluminum, stainless steel, chrome, rubber, plastics, glass, and sensor housings
Wash water
Use oil-water separation and solids capture; discharge only through the wash bay's permitted reclaim or sewer route.
