Task
Remove road film, bugs, grease, and aluminum oxidation without damaging fleet finishes or wash-bay controls.
Asset / substrate
Truck and bus exteriors, wheels, aluminum tanks, chassis, engine bays, wash-bay floors, and service equipment
Soil / deposit
Road film, insect residue, diesel soot, lubricating grease, brake dust, salt, and aluminum oxidation
Starting chemistry
VertKleen Torque, VertKleen CR HD, VertKleen MultiWash, VertKleen AlumiBrite
Concentration
Stay within the current wash/degreaser label range and establish a separate tested ratio for aluminum brightening; start with the weakest effective solution.
Process controls
Record water hardness, solution and panel temperature, dwell, brush or pressure agitation, rinse volume, and drying conditions.
Shutdown / containment
Keep chemistry off hot panels, brakes, sensors, polished finishes, and unapproved aluminum; follow fleet OEM and wash-equipment limits.
Verification endpoint
No road or oily film, no streaking after dry, gloss/finish acceptance, clean wheel test area, and no oily sheen in recovered water.
