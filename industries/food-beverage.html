Task

Asset / substrate
Process tanks, piping, fillers, pasteurizers, heat exchangers, kettles, conveyors, and removable parts
Soil / deposit
Fat, protein, carbohydrate, yeast, biofilm-supporting residue, beer or milk stone, and hard-water scale
Starting chemistry
VertKleen CIP CR, VertKleen CIP HCR, VertKleen CR HD, VertKleen Neutral, VertKleen MultiWash
Concentration
For the published CR/HCR CIP use, select 0.5–1.5 L per 10 gal from soil load and trial evidence; other products remain within their current labels.
Process controls
Log make-up concentration, temperature, turbulent flow or mechanical action, dwell/circulation time, conductivity, return condition, and final rinse pH.
Shutdown / containment
Use the facility SSOP, isolate product and allergen paths, verify food-contact materials, and never substitute a cleaning claim for sanitizing or disinfection.
Verification endpoint
No visible residue, rinse pH/conductivity at baseline, and the facility's ATP, protein/allergen, or microbiological acceptance result.
