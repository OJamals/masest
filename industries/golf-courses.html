Gym · fitness · studio & clinic cleaner

Label dilution / concentration

  • Equipment, machines & mats:  Dilute 1:32; mist onto a cloth or surface and wipe. Do not soak electronics
  • Floors & tile:  Dilute 5:1 in a mop bucket or auto-scrubber
  • Glass & mirrors:  Dilute 4:1 and wipe streak-free
  • Heavy soil & grout:  Dilute 2:1; apply, let stand, agitate and rinse

Task

Asset / substrate
Carts, mowers, utility vehicles, irrigation heads, pump components, shop floors, locker rooms, pavers, and fountains
Soil / deposit
Grass oil, grease, fertilizer salt, mineral scale, algae staining, mud, and tracked organic soil
Starting chemistry
VertKleen Torque, VertKleen LAM3, VertKleen CIP HCR, VertKleen MultiWash, Purgo
Concentration
Set each task within the current product label/TDS range; start weak near painted equipment, turf, water features, and irrigation elastomers.
Process controls
Record surface temperature, dilution, dwell, brush/low-pressure action, rinse requirement, and dry or return-to-play time.
Shutdown / containment
Protect turf, ponds, irrigation intakes, players, and battery/electrical components; separate cleaning from antimicrobial treatment.
Verification endpoint
Dry visual acceptance, no oily or slippery film, restored nozzle flow where relevant, and no visible turf or finish impact after the test area.
