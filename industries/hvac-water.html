Soil / deposit: Calcium carbonate, iron oxide, mixed mineral scale, silt, corrosion product, and organic fouling. Record current dissatisfaction, operating window, and acceptance endpoint before testing.

  2. Compatibility gate

    Materials in scope: Copper, carbon steel, stainless steel, aluminum, galvanized steel, tower polymers, elastomers, and treatment sensors. Use LOTO, bypass controls, ventilation, Legionella/water-management procedures, metallurgy checks, and treatment-vendor approval.

  3. Witnessed method

    Isolate, circulate or soak descaler, monitor reaction, drain, rinse, inspect, and recommission treatment. Select within the current HCR/user-guide range from deposit loading, metallurgy, volume, and reaction monitoring; treatment dose is site-engineered. Track concentration, solution temperature, flow/agitation, pH trend, iron/hardness response, dwell, rinse conductivity, and restart chemistry.

  4. Release and record

    Improved approach temperature, flow, or pressure drop; stable rinse pH/conductivity; clean inspection point; treatment residual back in range. Characterize spent solution for pH and metals; neutralize and discharge only through the approved facility route. Record the result and limitations.
