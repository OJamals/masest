Why VertKleen fits HVAC / Water Treatment.

Cooling-tower programs combine inhibitor, antimicrobial support, descaling acid, pH control, and sometimes a non-oxidizing biocide. VertKleen covers it with WaterSafe60, Purgo, HCR, and CR, with DBNPA footnoted as a low-hazard component when the non-oxidizing biocide is specified separately.
