Witnessed method

  1. Scope and baseline

    Asset / substrate: Warehouse floors, loading docks, forklifts, battery areas, drains, cold-room surfaces, and isolated evaporator components. Soil / deposit: Hydraulic oil, tire polymer, pallet grime, food residue, ice-related mineral scale, and drain film. Record current dissatisfaction, operating window, and acceptance endpoint before testing.

  2. Compatibility gate

    Materials in scope: Sealed concrete, epoxy, urethane, rubber tires, painted steel, stainless steel, aluminum, and drain components. Segregate food and packaging, isolate traffic and powered equipment, follow freezer/OEM limits, and do not represent cleaning as sanitizing.

  3. Witnessed method

    Sweep or scrape, pre-treat, low-foam auto-scrub or controlled foam, recover, and rinse. Use the current low-foam or general-cleaning label range; start weak, increase only after a slip-residue and coating test. Log floor temperature, dilution, wet dwell, pad/brush agitation, recovery passes, final rinse, and dry-to-traffic time.

  4. Release and record

    No visible oil or tire film, dry white-wipe, facility slip-resistance check, clear drains, and release only after the floor is dry. Recover scrubber solution; route grease and solids through the site's interceptor, pretreatment, and discharge schedule. Record the result and limitations.
