Applications and proof

Remove organic process soil and mineral film from plant or farm equipment before the site's sanitation or biosecurity step.

Broader program: Food & Beverage

Task
Remove organic process soil and mineral film from plant or farm equipment before the site's sanitation or biosecurity step.
Asset / substrate
Process vessels, lines, harvest equipment, bins, conveyors, wash-down areas, milking or beverage components, and drains
Soil / deposit
Fat, protein, starch, sugar, plant residue, fertilizer salt, manure-adjacent soil, and hard-water scale
Starting chemistry
VertKleen CIP CR, VertKleen CIP HCR, VertKleen CR HD Low Foam
Concentration
Use 0.5–1.5 L per 10 gal only for the applicable CR/HCR CIP direction; all other tasks require the current label range and a soil-specific trial.
Process controls
Track water and solution temperature, concentration, wet dwell, flow or brush action, rinse volume, return conductivity, and dry/inspection time.
Shutdown / containment
Separate raw and ready-to-eat zones, protect feed and food, follow allergen/biosecurity controls, and confirm food-contact rinse requirements.
Verification endpoint
Visual release plus the site's ATP, protein/allergen, or microbiological criterion; cleaning alone does not establish sanitation.
