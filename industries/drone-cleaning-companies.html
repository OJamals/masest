Witnessed method

  1. Scope and baseline

    Asset / substrate: Roofs, façades, siding, glazing, solar modules, canopies, and elevated coated surfaces. Soil / deposit: Atmospheric soot, algae and mildew staining, bird residue, salt film, and loose particulate. Record current dissatisfaction, operating window, and acceptance endpoint before testing.

  2. Compatibility gate

    Materials in scope: Coated metal, glass, roofing membrane, painted masonry, sealants, photovoltaic coatings, and landscaping. Follow aircraft and spray-system OEM limits, aviation rules, exclusion zones, overspray controls, landscaping protection, and electrical clearances.

  3. Witnessed method

    Ground-tested chemistry, calibrated low-pressure spray, controlled dwell, and rinse only where the label and substrate require it. Use the weakest current-label dilution that passes a ground-level finish and runoff test; never increase concentration to compensate for poor coverage. Record ambient/surface temperature, wind, nozzle flow, application rate, dwell, any rinse pass, flight path, and treated area.

  4. Release and record

    Matched-angle before/after images after drying, no streak or residue, coating/gloss check, and complete runoff-capture record. Map drainage before flight; block storm inlets and capture or divert runoff under the property permit. Record the result and limitations.
