Asset / substrate

Soil / deposit

Starting chemistry
VertKleen MultiWash, VertKleen LAM3, VertKleen Descaler, VertKleen Neutral
Concentration
Use the weakest current-label dilution that removes the target soil after a hidden-area finish, grout, sealant, and slip-residue test.
Process controls
Record dilution, surface temperature, dwell, agitation, rinse/recovery, ventilation, odor check, dry time, and area release.
Shutdown / containment
Barricade guest traffic, protect food/pools/landscaping, follow finish warranties, and keep cleaning separate from pool treatment and disinfection.
Verification endpoint
Dry visual and white-wipe acceptance, no streak or slippery film, neutral rinse where applicable, and no finish change in the test area.
