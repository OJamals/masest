Applications and job fit

Remove dust, bird residue, salt, and mineral spotting from modules without abrading glass or compromising energized equipment.

Task
Remove dust, bird residue, salt, and mineral spotting from modules without abrading glass or compromising energized equipment.
Asset / substrate
Photovoltaic module glass, frames, clamps, trackers, inverter-pad hardscape, and accessible support structures
Soil / deposit
Dust, pollen, bird residue, salt film, industrial fallout, light organic film, and hard-water spotting
Starting chemistry
VertKleen MultiWash, VertKleen LAM3
Concentration
Use the weakest current-label dilution that passes the module maker's coating and residue test; water-only remains the baseline where OEM-required.
Process controls
Record irradiance/weather, glass temperature, water conductivity, dilution, dwell, brush pressure, rinse volume, and drying condition.
Shutdown / containment
Follow module/O&M warranty, energized-work, fall, tracker, coating, and electrical-connector restrictions; avoid thermal shock and dry cleaning abrasion.
Success check
Spot-free dry glass, matched-angle imaging, no coating/scratch change, and normalized before/after power or soiling-ratio trend when available.
