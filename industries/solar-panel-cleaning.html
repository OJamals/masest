On site

How the work is run on site.

Set the job up the way the crew will actually run it, then check the finished result against the same marks every time.

Dilution
Use the weakest current-label dilution that passes the module maker's coating and residue test; water-only remains the baseline where OEM-required.
What to log
Record irradiance/weather, glass temperature, water conductivity, dilution, working time, brush pressure, rinse volume, and drying condition.
Site controls
Follow module/O&M warranty, energized-work, fall, tracker, coating, and electrical-connector restrictions; avoid thermal shock and dry cleaning abrasion.
Finished-result check
Spot-free dry glass, matched-angle imaging, no coating/scratch change, and normalized before/after power or grimeing-ratio trend when available.
Surfaces
Tempered coated glass, anodized aluminum, sealants, backsheet edges, cable jackets, plastics, and painted/galvanized supports
Wash water
Map runoff, protect grime and waterways, and capture chemical or contaminated rinse where the site permit requires it.
