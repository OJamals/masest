On site

How the work is run on site.

Set the job up the way the crew will actually run it, then check the finished result against the same marks every time.

Dilution
Set cleaning concentration from the current label/user guide, buildup sample, metallurgy, and asset volume; treatment dosing is utility-engineered.
What to log
Track concentration, solution temperature, flow/scrubbing, pH, working time, metal response, rinse conductivity/turbidity, and controlled restart.
Site controls
Use LOTO, confined-space rules, cross-connection control, permit requirements, and the utility's separate disinfection and sampling plan for potable assets.
Finished-result check
Clean inspection point, restored flow or pressure drop, stable rinse pH/conductivity/turbidity, then required utility bacteriological release.
Surfaces
Ductile iron, carbon and stainless steel, copper, concrete, coatings, PVC/HDPE, elastomers, and treatment instruments
Wash water
Characterize pH, metals, solids, and treatment residuals; route only under the utility's NPDES, sewer, or hauled-waste authorization.
