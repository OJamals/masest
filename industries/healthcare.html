On site

How the work is run on site.

Set the job up the way the crew will actually run it, then check the finished result against the same marks every time.

Dilution
Set descaling concentration from the current label/user guide, buildup analysis, and metallurgy; all treatment dosing remains under the facility water plan.
What to log
Record temperature, concentration, flow/scrubbing, contact time, rinse pH/conductivity, ventilation, barricade, and restart readings.
Site controls
Use infection-control risk assessment, LOTO, ASHRAE 188/water-management controls, and facility-approved disinfectants. Cleaning is not disinfection.
Finished-result check
Mechanical trend recovery plus neutral/stable rinse; environmental ATP or microbiological release only when required and interpreted by the facility.
Surfaces
Copper, stainless and carbon steel, aluminum, elastomers, coated tower parts, tile, sealed flooring, and approved polymers
Wash water
Characterize pH, metals, and biocide/treatment residuals; route under hospital EHS and wastewater authorization.
