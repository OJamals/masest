VertKlean Neutral, VertKlean MultiWash, VertKlean Descaler
Concentration
Use the lowest current-label dilution that passes a finish and residue test; descaling ratios require fixture metallurgy and deposit review.
Process controls
Document dilution, surface temperature, wet dwell, agitation, rinse/recovery, ventilation, dry time, and reoccupancy release.

Coordinate infection prevention, resident protection, food service, ventilation, fall prevention, and chemical storage. No disinfectant claim is borrowed.
Success check
No visible soil or film, dry white-wipe, acceptable slip condition, rinse baseline for descaling, then the facility's separate hygiene endpoint.
