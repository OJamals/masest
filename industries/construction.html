On site

How the work is run on site.

Set the job up the way the crew will actually run it, then check the finished result against the same marks every time.

Dilution
Choose the weakest current-label dilution that removes the identified cement, oil, or mineral grime after a hidden-area compatibility test.
What to log
Record surface temperature, controlled wet soak, brush or low-pressure scrubbing, and rinse volume; prevent mist and never dry-grind a chemical-cleaning residue.
Site controls
Follow silica controls, equipment OEM limits, coating compatibility, stormwater plans, and site containment. Do not acid-clean uncured or architecturally sensitive concrete without approval.
Finished-result check
No visible cement or oily film, clean white-wipe where practical, acceptable finish in the test area, and documented wash-water recovery.
Surfaces
Carbon steel, aluminum, hydraulic seals, painted equipment, cured concrete, masonry, and form coatings
Wash water
Collect slurry and wash water; settle solids and route liquid under the site's SWPPP and local discharge authorization.
