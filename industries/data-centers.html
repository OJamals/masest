On site

How the work is run on site.

Set the job up the way the crew will actually run it, then check the finished result against the same marks every time.

Dilution
Set descaler concentration from the current label/user guide, buildup analysis, metallurgy, and loop volume; water-treatment dose remains site-engineered.
What to log
Trend inlet/return temperature, flow, pressure drop, solution pH, iron or hardness response, contact time, scrubbing/recirculation, and rinse conductivity.
Site controls
Use MOP/SOP/EOP change control, redundancy approval, leak containment, LOTO, and zero open chemical work above live electrical or IT equipment.
Finished-result check
Recovered approach temperature or pressure drop, stable rinse pH/conductivity, clean strainer inspection, and no leak or alarm after controlled restart.
Surfaces
Copper, stainless steel, carbon steel, aluminum coils, elastomers, treatment probes, and coated tower components
Wash water
Capture spent cleanout solution; characterize metals and pH before routing through the facility's approved wastewater path.
