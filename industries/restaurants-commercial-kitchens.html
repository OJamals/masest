Broader program: Food & Beverage

Task
Remove polymerized fat, grease, and carbon from kitchen assets before the required rinse and sanitation step.
Asset / substrate
Hoods, filters, fryers kept out unless OEM-approved, floors, walls, equipment exteriors, grease traps, drains, and wash areas
Soil / deposit
Animal and vegetable fat, polymerized grease, carbonized food, protein film, floor oil, and drain residue
Starting chemistry
VertKleen CR HD, Purgo, VertKleen MultiWash, VertKleen Neutral
Concentration
Use the current CR HD/MultiWash label range; start at the weakest dilution that passes stainless, aluminum, grout, and slip-residue tests.
Process controls
Record surface and solution temperature, dilution, dwell, foam/brush action, rinse temperature/volume, recovery, and dry time.
Shutdown / containment
Shut down heat and ignition, protect food, isolate hood fire systems as authorized, verify aluminum, and keep cleaning separate from sanitizing.
Verification endpoint
No visible grease, clean white cloth, no slippery film after dry, clear drain flow, then the kitchen's separate sanitation release.
