Witnessed method

  1. Scope and baseline

    Asset / substrate: Hoods, filters, fryers kept out unless OEM-approved, floors, walls, equipment exteriors, grease traps, drains, and wash areas. Soil / deposit: Animal and vegetable fat, polymerized grease, carbonized food, protein film, floor oil, and drain residue. Record current dissatisfaction, operating window, and acceptance endpoint before testing.

  2. Compatibility gate

    Materials in scope: Stainless steel, aluminum only after test, tile, grout, sealed flooring, plastics, rubber, paint, and hood components. Shut down heat and ignition, protect food, isolate hood fire systems as authorized, verify aluminum, and keep cleaning separate from sanitizing.

  3. Witnessed method

    Cool and isolate, scrape bulk soil, apply degreaser, control dwell, brush/foam, hot rinse where allowed, and recover. Use the current CR HD/MultiWash label range; start at the weakest dilution that passes stainless, aluminum, grout, and slip-residue tests. Record surface and solution temperature, dilution, dwell, foam/brush action, rinse temperature/volume, recovery, and dry time.

  4. Release and record

    No visible grease, clean white cloth, no slippery film after dry, clear drain flow, then the kitchen's separate sanitation release. Scrape solids first; route fats/oils/grease through the interceptor and approved sanitary drain, never stormwater. Record the result and limitations.
