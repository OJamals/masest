Clean brewery organics first. Remove beer stone second.

CR clears krausen, protein, yeast, and organic film. HCR follows with beer stone, rust, and mineral scale for a complete two-step CIP clean.

  • CleanFermenters, bright tanks, kettles, fillers, kegs, transfer lines, and plate heat exchangers
  • RemoveKrausen, protein, carbohydrate film, yeast, beer stone, tartrate, and mineral scale
  • Start withRinse first, circulate CR, rinse again, then use HCR when beer stone or scale is present. Finish with the brewery's normal sanitation step
