Broader program: Food & Beverage

Task
Run an alkaline-and-acid CIP sequence against organic film, beer stone, and mineral scale in process circuits.
Asset / substrate
Fermenters, bright tanks, kettles, fillers, kegs, transfer lines, and plate heat exchangers
Soil / deposit
Krausen, protein, carbohydrate film, yeast, beer stone, tartrate, and mineral scale
Starting chemistry
VertKleen CIP CR, VertKleen CIP HCR, VertKleen CR HD Low Foam
Concentration
Current CIP directions use 0.5–1.5 L per 10 gal; select within that range from soil load, circuit volume, temperature, and a witnessed trial.
Process controls
Log make-up concentration, return temperature, turbulent flow, circulation time, conductivity, rinse pH, and visual condition at the return.
Shutdown / containment
Isolate the circuit, verify 316 stainless/PVC/gasket compatibility, prevent cross-connections, and keep cleaning separate from the facility's validated sanitizing step.
Verification endpoint
Rinse-return pH and conductivity at baseline, no visible soil at inspection points, plus the plant's ATP, allergen, or microbiological release test.
