Witnessed method

  1. Scope and baseline

    Asset / substrate: Fermenters, bright tanks, kettles, fillers, kegs, transfer lines, and plate heat exchangers. Soil / deposit: Krausen, protein, carbohydrate film, yeast, beer stone, tartrate, and mineral scale. Record current dissatisfaction, operating window, and acceptance endpoint before testing.

  2. Compatibility gate

    Materials in scope: 316 stainless steel, PVC, approved hoses, pump seals, valve seats, and heat-exchanger gaskets. Isolate the circuit, verify 316 stainless/PVC/gasket compatibility, prevent cross-connections, and keep cleaning separate from the facility's validated sanitizing step.

  3. Witnessed method

    Validated CIP: pre-rinse, alkaline circulation, intermediate rinse, acid circulation when required, final rinse, then the site's sanitation step. Current CIP directions use 0.5–1.5 L per 10 gal; select within that range from soil load, circuit volume, temperature, and a witnessed trial. Log make-up concentration, return temperature, turbulent flow, circulation time, conductivity, rinse pH, and visual condition at the return.

  4. Release and record

    Rinse-return pH and conductivity at baseline, no visible soil at inspection points, plus the plant's ATP, allergen, or microbiological release test. Neutralize and discharge only within the site's pretreatment permit, pH limits, organic loading controls, and volume schedule. Record the result and limitations.
