Broader program: Manufacturing

Task
Remove hydraulic fluid, grease, and exhaust soil from ground-support equipment and approved aircraft-adjacent components.
Asset / substrate
Ground-support equipment, shop floors, wheel-well tooling, engine stands, and approved aluminum or coated parts
Soil / deposit
Hydraulic oil, lubricating grease, carbon film, rubber transfer, and ramp grime
Starting chemistry
VertKleen CR HD, VertKleen Neutral, VertKleen AlumiBrite
Concentration
Use only the current CR HD or Neutral label/TDS range; begin at the weakest effective dilution after an aluminum, coating, and elastomer coupon test.
Process controls
Record solution temperature, wet dwell, brush or parts-washer agitation, rinse-water quality, and dry time; never let chemistry dry on the surface.
Shutdown / containment
Use the operator's maintenance manual, material approvals, lockout, FOD controls, and wash-water capture. No application to flight-critical parts without written engineering approval.
Verification endpoint
Dry visual inspection under task lighting, white-wipe or water-break check where approved, and corrosion/coating inspection before return to service.
