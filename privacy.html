Your privacy, in plain English.

Updated July 9, 2026

When you ask a question, request a quote, create an account, or place an order, you share information with us. We use it to help you, complete your order, and make the site work better. We do not sell it.

What we collect

We may collect your name, company, email, phone, shipping and billing addresses, job details, product interests, messages, document requests, quotes, orders, invoices, and account information.

Our site also collects basic technical information, such as the pages you visit, your browser and device, how you found us, and an approximate location. Chat may include your message and the page you were viewing.

Payments

Our payment provider handles card details. MASEST receives the information needed to confirm payment, prevent fraud, and fulfill your order, but the website does not store your full card number.

Why we use it

We use your information to answer questions, recommend products, prepare quotes, process and ship orders, run your account, send requested files, support invoices, prevent abuse, improve the website, and send marketing only when you opt in.

Who helps us

Trusted providers help us run hosting, security, analytics, email, chat, payments, accounting, shipping, fulfillment, and customer support. They receive only the information needed to do that work. We may also share information when the law requires it or when needed to protect customers, MASEST, or the site.

How long we keep it

We keep customer and order information as long as we need it for service, accounting, taxes, security, and business records. Some information may remain in invoices, transaction history, logs, or backups after an account changes.

Your choices

Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. To see, correct, download, or delete your information, email matthew@masest.co from the address tied to your account or company. We may still need to keep order, tax, payment, or security records.

A note for parents

This is a business website, not a site for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Questions or changes

Questions are welcome at matthew@masest.co. If this page changes, the updated date above will change too.
