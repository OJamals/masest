Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

MASEST Consulting LLC uses buyer and site data to respond to VertKleen requests, support procurement, operate account and checkout workflows, maintain records, and improve the website.

Scope

This policy applies to masest.co, MASEST account and dashboard pages, quote and contact forms, newsletter signups, checkout flows, document requests, live chat, and related support conversations. Separate written quotes, invoices, distributor agreements, or customer contracts may add more data terms for a specific transaction.

Information MASEST collects

Forms and account workflows may collect name, company, job title, email, phone, addresses, industry, application notes, product interest, volume, timing, tax or resale details, account setup details, order records, invoice status, document requests, buyer messages, and support history.

Checkout and payment workflows may collect order, billing, shipping, tax, fraud-prevention, and payment-status details. MASEST does not intentionally store complete payment card numbers on the website.

Device, analytics, and chat data

The site may collect page, referral, browser, device, approximate location, and event data through first-party analytics, hosting logs, security tools, newsletter tooling, and chat features. Live chat and support tools may process conversation content, contact details, and page context so MASEST can respond to buyer questions.

How MASEST uses information

MASEST uses information to answer requests, recommend products, prepare quotes, process orders, manage accounts, route SDS and product documents, support invoices and accounting, send operational messages, send opted-in marketing, measure site performance, protect against abuse, and comply with legal or tax obligations.

How information is shared

MASEST does not sell personal information. Data may be shared with vendors that help operate hosting, security, analytics, email, newsletter, chat, payment, accounting, CRM, shipping, fulfillment, and customer support workflows. Data may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights or security, or as part of a business transfer.

Retention

MASEST keeps records for as long as needed for customer service, procurement history, account administration, accounting, tax, legal, security, and operational purposes. Some information may remain in backups, logs, invoices, or transaction records even after an account or contact record is changed.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in those emails. You can request access, correction, export, or deletion by emailing matthew@masest.co with the email address or company name tied to the request. MASEST may need to retain certain records for tax, order, fraud-prevention, contract, or legal reasons.

Children

The site is intended for business and procurement use. It is not directed to children under 13, and MASEST does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

MASEST may update this policy as the website, account tools, vendor stack, or legal requirements change. The updated date above shows when the current version took effect.
