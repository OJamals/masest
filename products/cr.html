Match the dose to the soil.

Start with the CIP CR label directions for your soil load. Measure the product, circulate through the equipment, then rinse with water.

VertKleen CIP CR label dilutions
Soil loadProduct per 10 gal
Light / krausen soil0.5 L

Severe soil1.5 L

For light / krausen soil, the label specifies hot circulation above 140°F. Set circulation time and temperature for your equipment and cleaning endpoint; confirm the cycle with your brewery's verification procedure.

Build a repeatable brewery wash.

  1. 1. Set up the circuitDrain product and remove loose solids. Check the tank, pump, seals, hoses, and heat exchanger for the planned wash temperature and chemistry.
  2. 2. Dose and circulateChoose the label dose for the soil load. Circulate to wet the full cleaning surface, including the return loop and difficult deposits.
  3. 3. Rinse and inspectRinse with water and verify soil removal. CR needs no separate acid-neutralizing step; use HCR separately when mineral deposits need removal.
  4. 4. Verify before productionComplete your brewery's sanitation and release procedure. Record concentration, temperature, circulation time, rinse endpoint, and the verification result for the next cycle.

Brewlando: a 25-minute CR wash.

After roughly 10 days without CIP cleaning, the mash tank carried a heavy residue ring and bottom deposits. The trial mixed 5 L of CR into 55 gal of water at 160–170°F and circulated through the tank and heat exchanger for 25 minutes. The brewmaster judged the tank very clean. A separate 30-minute HCR step followed before the final rinse.
