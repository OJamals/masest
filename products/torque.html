Apply. Brush. Rinse to finish.

  1. 1. Choose your wash methodApply the label dilution by hand, bucket and brush, or a foaming applicator. Work across the areas carrying dirt, grease, salt, and bug residue.
  2. 2. Brush where neededAgitate stubborn deposits and heavily soiled areas to help release the film from the surface.
  3. 3. Rinse wellA thorough water rinse carries away loosened grime and distributes the wax and anti-stick coating. The finish is part of the wash.
