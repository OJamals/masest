Dose to target. Verify the result.

  1. 1. Define the processStart with the liquid being treated, current and target pH, temperature, and batch volume or flow rate. Include the sulfuric acid concentration and feed rate you use today.
  2. 2. Bench-titrate your fluidTest a representative sample to establish the SAR dose needed for the target pH. Use that measured demand to plan the batch addition or metering rate.
  3. 3. Match the feed hardwareThe SAR label recommends 316 stainless steel, polypropylene, or Schedule 80 PVC. Do not use aluminum fittings or piping. Confirm tanks, hoses, seals, and connections for the application.
  4. 4. Add, mix, and measureAdd SAR to the process liquid, mix, and check pH as you approach the target. Confirm the result before adopting the treatment rate for routine operation.
