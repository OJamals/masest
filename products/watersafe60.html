NSF/ANSI/CAN 60 certified.

Watersafe 60 is listed for drinking-water treatment and offline water-system maintenance under NSF/ANSI/CAN 60.

Request WaterSafe60 certification documentation

  • Metered treatmentListed for corrosion and scale control, descaling, and pH adjustment. Maximum use level: 80 mg/L.
  • Offline applicationsListed for descaling, drilling fluid, pipe cleaning, well cleaning, well drilling, and well rehabilitation. Flush the product out before returning the system to drinking-water service.

Metered treatment. Controlled at the pump.

Set the feed rate from water quality, treatment demand, and the target result.

  • Scale and corrosion controlThe technical sheet gives 2–10 mg/L for normal or below-normal hardness. Start low, inspect the system, and adjust within the listed maximum.
  • pH adjustmentBench-titrate a representative sample. Use upstream and downstream pH measurements to verify the response at the injection point.
  • 80 mg/L maximumThis is the NSF maximum use level for the listed treatment functions, not a default operating dose.

Offline cleaning. Isolate, treat, and flush.

Use a dedicated cleaning cycle for pipe deposits, fouled equipment, and well rehabilitation.

  • Separate the cleaning circuitShut off or disconnect the section being treated. Select the cleaning concentration for the deposit load and application using the product directions.
  • Give the treatment time to workThe technical sheet specifies at least 30 minutes for offline pipe cleaning, descaling, and well rehabilitation. Match the full procedure to the equipment and condition.
  • Restore water serviceDrain or purge the treated system and flush with fresh water. Verify that pH has returned to normal before reconnecting to drinking-water service.

Titrate your water. Dial in the dose.

The published Sigma sample report starts at pH 8 and shows estimated additions for several target pH levels. It illustrates why the starting water matters. Establish your own treatment demand while staying within the applicable use limit.
