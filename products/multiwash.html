  1. 1. Prepare the jobRemove loose debris, protect nearby items, and test the working dilution on a small area. Identify the package label and the equipment you will use.
  2. 2. Apply the solutionUse a cloth, mop, brush, sprayer, or compatible foam applicator. Wet the dirty surface evenly without soaking electrical components.
  3. 3. Give it time to workFollow the package's contact-time directions and agitate stubborn areas. Keep the application from drying on the surface during active cleaning.
  4. 4. Rinse and recoverRinse away loosened residue and recover the dirty solution. Inspect the finish, then record dilution, contact time, passes, and product used.
