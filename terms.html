Use MASEST.co to learn about VertKleen, ask questions, get quotes, manage your account, and buy available products. Please use the site honestly and give us accurate information.

Choosing and using a product

Website copy gives you a useful starting point. Before use, read the current label and SDS, make sure the product fits your surface and job, and test a small area when appropriate. If anything is unclear, ask us.

Prices, quotes, and orders

Small packs can be bought online when shown as available. Drums, totes, glycol, distributor orders, and some special products are quoted. Stock, shipping, tax, discounts, and payment terms are final when we confirm the order or quote.

Your account

Keep your contact, company, shipping, and payment information accurate. Protect your sign-in details. We may pause access if we see fraud, abuse, a payment problem, or a security risk.

Using the site fairly

Do not break into the site, bypass access controls, overload it, submit malicious code, misuse payment information, or copy the catalog to build a competing service.

If something is wrong

We work to keep prices, product details, links, and account tools accurate and available. Mistakes and outages can happen. Report a problem to matthew@masest.co and we will help.
