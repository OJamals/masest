Shipping and returns.

Updated September 2026

When your order ships, where we deliver, and what to do if something is not right. Questions go to sales@masest.co.

Where we ship

Online orders ship to street addresses in the 48 contiguous states and Washington, DC. We cannot deliver online orders to Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories, military (APO, FPO, and DPO) addresses, or PO boxes. For any of those, request a quote and we will price the delivery for you.

When it ships

Orders placed by 2 pm Eastern on a business day ship the next business day from our Florida warehouse. Checkout shows live carrier rates and an estimated delivery date before you pay.

Drums, totes, and other bulk sizes ship by freight. They are quoted, and the quote sets the delivery terms.

Cancel before it ships

Email sales@masest.co with your order number before the order ships, and we will cancel it and refund you in full.

Returns

You can return unopened products in their original sealed containers within 30 days of delivery. For safety, we cannot accept opened containers. Email sales@masest.co with your order number first, and we will tell you where to send it.

You pay return shipping. We refund the product price to your original payment method within 5 business days of receiving the return, and your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to post it. Original shipping charges are not refunded. There is no restocking fee.

Damaged, leaking, or wrong items

If an order arrives damaged, leaking, or wrong, email sales@masest.co within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We will send a replacement or refund you, at our cost, and you will not need to send the product back. Do not use product from a damaged or leaking container.

Payment and NET terms

Online orders are paid by card or bank transfer (ACH) at checkout, processed securely by Stripe. Approved business accounts can pay quoted orders on NET terms. NET invoices of $2,500 or more are paid by ACH bank transfer.

Orders placed through a quote follow the terms in that quote.
