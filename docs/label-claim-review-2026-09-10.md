# Unsubstantiated health claims in the document library — for label-authority review

Compiled 2026-09-10. **Nothing here is applied, and no PDF has been modified.**

This is a review sheet for whoever holds label and regulatory sign-off. The proposed
wording is a starting point from someone who read the SDS, not a legal opinion, and I am
not asserting that any replacement is sufficient for any particular jurisdiction.

## What the SDS say

All twelve VertKleen SDS carry an identical GHS classification:

| | classification | hazard statement |
|---|---|---|
| oral | **Acute Toxicity Category 5** | "May be harmful if swallowed" |
| skin | **Skin Corrosion/Irritation Category 3** | "Causes mild skin irritation" |
| eyes | **Eye Damage/Eye Irritation Category 2B** | "Causes eye irritation" |
| | Signal Word | **Warning** |

Two claim types contradict that:

- **"non-toxic"** — an absolute claim against a Category 5 classification
- **"safe on skin / eyes"** — a contact-safety claim against Category 3 and 2B

**Not included, deliberately.** Six occurrences of "non-toxic" appear as a result band from
a cited test method (OECD 202, LC50/LD50, rat oral) and three describe a proprietary
*ingredient* rather than the product. Those are substantiated in context and are left
alone. An oral LD50 result cannot substantiate a skin- or eye-contact claim, so that
exemption was applied to the toxicity wording only.

## What is still supported, and safe to use as replacement wording

HMIS 0-0-0 (stated in all twelve), **non-corrosive** (Category 3 is an irritant
classification, not a corrosive one, and the word "corrosive" appears nowhere in the
twelve), **non-flammable** (flammability 0), ships non-hazmat, and "no product-specific PPE
or ventilation requirement for routine use".

## Three things that came up alongside, not actioned

1. **`vertkleen-lam3-tds.pdf` claims "Safe on Skin/Pets".** Nothing in the twelve SDS
   addresses animal exposure. The pet half of that claim is unsupported by any document
   reviewed here, and it is a distinct claim from the skin one.
2. **`watersafe60-cr-nsf60-user-guide.pdf` states "The pH of BlowOut is zero"** immediately
   beside a skin-safety heading. A pH-0 product and a skin-safety claim are difficult to
   hold together; worth a look independent of this audit.
3. **The same user guide contains typos** ("competetive", "reaplcements") — cosmetic, but
   it is a customer-facing document.

## Not audited

Website HTML outside the blog, email templates, JS and CMS content were scanned and are
clean. Physical printed stock already in circulation, packaging artwork not present in this
repository, and any distributor or marketplace listing were **not** examined.
