# VertKleen Exact-Chemical Affiliate Authority Review

Date: 2026-08-22

## Scope correction

VertKleen Purgo, CR, and CR HD are private-label presentations of the same exact chemicals sold under identified Environmental Manufacturing Solutions (EMS), SynClean/Syntech, and affiliate names. They are not merely members of a related brand family. This exact-formula identity is owner-confirmed project authority.

Public documents corroborate the technical crosswalk, but do not replace a current manufacturer equivalence letter, revision-controlled master formula record, or product-specific registration.

## Corrected determination

1. **Purgo is an antimicrobial product.** The current US claim basis found is FIFRA section 25(b) minimum-risk exemption, not federal EPA registration or EPA certification. EMS separately identifies Purgo N as its EPA-registered disinfectant/sanitizer.
2. **Exact-chemical affiliate technical data is relevant.** SDS/TDS, physical properties, ingredients, and test data may substantiate VertKleen product documentation after revision, concentration, and finished-product reconciliation.
3. **Affiliate marketing claims do not transfer automatically.** Brand registration, jurisdiction, label directions, organisms, contact time, public-health claims, NSF/DIN permissions, and trademarks remain product- and jurisdiction-specific.
4. **Existing local Purgo SDS is not acceptable.** It is effectively the VertKleen CR SDS with only product/use/revision text changed. CR-specific physical properties and high-pH spill language remain. Exact-chemical affiliate Purgo/Xtreme Bio data materially differs.
5. **Marine antimicrobial label needs revision, not product withdrawal.** Keep antimicrobial positioning. Remove misleading EPA-certification implication and absolute safety claims; add full identity, ingredients, directions, dilution/contact time, and bounded tested claim conditions.

## Local-document defects

### P0 — Replace Purgo SDS

`docs/sds/vertkleen-purgo-sds.pdf` duplicates `docs/sds/vertkleen-cr-sds.pdf` except product name, use, and revision/copyright text. It retains CR/high-pH content, including:

- high-pH spill-neutralization language;
- specific gravity `1.24 ± .02`;
- freezing point `≤ -10 °F (23 °C)`; the parenthetical conversion is also wrong;
- boiling point `≥ 234 °F`;
- oral LD50 `2051 mg/kg`.

Exact-chemical affiliate Purgo/Xtreme Bio SDS instead reports approximately:

- water-based spill cleanup;
- specific gravity `1.00 ± .02`;
- freezing point `≤ 32 °F / 0 °C`;
- boiling point `≥ 211 °F / 99 °C`;
- oral LD50 `3120 mg/kg`.

Action: obtain current EMS master SDS for the exact VertKleen Purgo formula/revision; replace local PDF; update website SDS metadata and checksums; retire copied CR artifact.

### P1 — Rebuild marine antimicrobial label

Current label uses `EPA TRIPLE-ZERO`, `KILLS 99.9%`, `Safe for All Marine Surfaces`, and `Safe for All Marine Environments`, but lacks full ingredient/direction context.

Action:

- title: `FIFRA 25(b)-exempt antimicrobial multi-surface cleaner`;
- remove `EPA Triple-Zero` and EPA-approval implication;
- replace absolute safety claims with bounded compatibility/environment wording supported by master label/SDS;
- include active/inert ingredients, responsible company, net contents, storage/disposal, dilution, application method, exact contact time, and rinse requirements;
- use `99.9%` only for named, permitted non-public-health target and exact tested use conditions;
- if secondary-container label, explicitly identify it as such and preserve required master-label linkage.

### P1 — Create canonical exact-chemical equivalence registry

Record one revision-controlled source of truth:

Manufacturer equivalence letter should state exact formula, concentration, manufacturing site, effective revision, and whether finished-product test results transfer unchanged.

## Bounded claim decision

Approved direction:

> VertKleen Purgo is a FIFRA 25(b)-exempt antimicrobial cleaner formulated to control odor-causing bacteria and other non-public-health microorganisms as directed on the label.

Do not publish without additional product-specific authority:

- `EPA certified`, `EPA approved`, or `EPA registered` for Purgo;
- `disinfects`, `sanitizes`, `sterilizes`, `kills germs`, or human-pathogen claims;
- generic `kills 99.9%` without target, dilution, contact time, method, and finished-product test support;
- `safe for all surfaces`, `safe for all environments`, `non-toxic`, or equivalent absolutes;
- Purgo N registration claims applied to Purgo.

## Final verdict

Prior finding that treated Synclean/Syntech/Purgo materials as a loose brand-family analogy is withdrawn. Exact-chemical equivalence is accepted. Highest-priority issue is now document integrity: current VertKleen Purgo SDS is a CR-derived mismatch and marine label overreaches permitted/conditioned claim scope. Replace SDS, reconcile exact formula revision, rebuild label, then update site claims and downloads from canonical equivalence registry.
