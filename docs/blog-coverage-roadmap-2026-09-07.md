# MASEST blog coverage roadmap — 2026-09-07

## Coverage basis

This plan follows the current industry-application inventory: **26 industry records** and six job paths. Each row lists every job path assigned to that record.

- **Existing** — a published article directly addresses the sector or its core job.
- **Partial** — a published article covers a related task or asset, without presenting itself as a sector case study.
- **New** — a dedicated draft is ready in the current refresh package.
- **Gap** — no published or dedicated-draft article covers the sector's task mix.

## Newsletter use

Send each article as a job-specific module: lead with the maintenance moment, link to the industry page and relevant product, then offer the most closely related guide as the next read. Segment by facility operations, EHS/compliance, procurement, and service contractors when the message is relevant to that role.

Use a published case study only for the sector or asset it actually documents. A general proof library should remain labeled as general industrial results, never as evidence for an uncovered industry.
