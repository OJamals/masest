# AlumiBrite: external research and page direction

Reviewed 2026-09-25. The owner confirmed that MASEST directly resells the same EMS chemical and authorizes free website use of EMS technical sheets, logos, studies, comparisons, and supporting records. This note distinguishes that formulation from unrelated aluminum cleaners with similar names. Document dates below come from the documents themselves; a search-engine crawl date is not a revision date.

## Product identity and strongest positioning

The manufacturer identifies the product as **EMS Alumi-Brite**, an aluminum cleaner and brightener. EMS's current product page places it in the ready-mix range and emphasizes cleaning that restores aluminum brightness without etching. That gives MASEST a more specific story than general-purpose metal cleaning: **bring back aluminum's natural brightness across trucks, trailers, and equipment.** [EMS product page, undated; accessed 2026-09-25](https://www.enviromfg.com/products1)

RussTech publishes an EMS-branded SDS naming Environmental Manufacturing Solutions and the industrial aluminum-cleaning use. Its revision date is **2015-05-20**. This establishes EMS product identity; the owner separately confirmed the MASEST supply relationship. [EMS Alumi-Brite SDS, hosted by RussTech](https://www.russtech.com/uploads/sds/Alumi-Brite-SDS.pdf)

Suggested lead: “Bring back aluminum's natural brightness. AlumiBrite cleans trucks, trailers, and equipment with a non-etching formula made for routine fleet care.” This is proposed marketing language, grounded in the manufacturer positioning above.

## Current manufacturer-linked technical document

The Alumi-Brite link on the current EMS product page opens [EMS Alumi-Brite technical data](https://www.enviromfg.com/s/alumibritewso-tds.pdf). The two-page document carries **©2022**, not a formal revision date. The same file is served from [EMS's Squarespace asset URL](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5dee8a7c57e1833f5c2ae5d3/t/622a2f697519a6176d339e08/1646931819444/alumibritewso-tds.pdf).

Useful product facts:

- SynTech synthetic-acid technology; formulated without hydrofluoric acid.
- Paint and glass compatibility; non-fuming; one cleaning step without separate neutralization.
- No VOCs or phosphates reported; water-soluble; one-year shelf life.
- SynGuard provides resistance to subsequent stain adhesion; no protection duration specified.
- Manufacturer comparison reports brightening scores of **90.1 for Alumi-Brite, 92.5 for HF, and 86.3 for HCl**. Conditions: 200 grams of 5% active solution; five square inches of corroded 7075-Y6 aluminum; three minutes at 70°F. Scores are not removal percentages, field guarantees, or application directions.
- Dilution instruction now defers to the product label.

Use the benchmark only with its conditions and manufacturer attribution. Keep the document downloadable beside the explanation.

## Distributor ideas worth adapting

**Synpro / Riviera Brush:** focuses on recurring fleet appearance, metal brightness, paint/glass compatibility, and fewer process steps. Its July 28, 2017 article names SynTech and SynGuard; it also mixes the names Alumi-Brite and Redi-Brite, so use its positioning rather than assuming exact equivalence across every brand. Best adaptation: explain how the cleaner fits truck-care work, with simple “clean, brighten, rinse” language and a separate label-guidance link. Do not repeat its universal superiority or absolute personal-safety statements. [Synpro article](https://synproproducts.com/tired-aluminum-chrome-trucks-trailors-looking-dull-grey/), [Synpro product sheet, undated; stored in a 2017 upload path](https://synproproducts.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/Alumibrite.pdf)

**RussTech:** leads with the aluminum-specific difference: brightening without the etching that can leave metal dull. Trucks and trailers anchor the application story. This supports a clear fleet-maintenance application card and aluminum-focused photo caption. [RussTech Synthetic Acids / EMS page, undated; accessed 2026-09-25](https://www.russtech.com/surface-products/synthetic-acids)

**EMS Slovenia:** extends the same EMS/SynTech identity to industrial equipment and wheels, with natural appearance as the main benefit. Useful for organizing applications; its broad environmental and personal-safety language should not become new MASEST promises. [EMS Slovenia Alumi Brite page, undated; accessed 2026-09-25](https://www.ems-slo.si/alumi-brite-sredstvo-za-ciscenje-in-osvetlitev-aluminija-brez-kisline)

**1st Ayd:** its EMS technical flyer identifies SKU 989. Its catalog separates this product from SKU 990, a hydrofluoric/sulfuric-acid cleaner. This is a concrete example of why neighboring aluminum-cleaner listings cannot supply directions for MASEST AlumiBrite. [989 technical flyer, ©2022](https://1stayd.com/images/uploaded/989%20Alumi-Brite%20TDS%20Flyer.pdf), [1st Ayd catalog, accessed 2026-09-25](https://1stayd.com/images/uploaded/1st%20Ayd%20Catalog.pdf)

## Application guidance and document conflicts

Older RussTech literature lists 10:1 for well-kept equipment, 5:1 for moderate soil, 3:1 for heavy soil, and undiluted for severe soil; spray/brush application followed by a 2–3-minute dwell and rinse. Its attached MSDS is revised **2004-05-05** and the technical section lists three-year storage stability. These legacy directions should not override the newer manufacturer-linked sheet's label instruction. [Older EMS literature hosted by RussTech](https://www.russtech.com/uploads/product-notes/Alumi-Brite-Literature.pdf)

Recommended buyer-facing guidance: “Choose the dilution and contact time on your product label, apply evenly, and rinse thoroughly. Need a routine for your fleet or equipment? Ask MASEST for application guidance.” No invented ratio orientation, universal dwell, coverage, or ready-to-use cost calculator.

Parent review also found conflicting HMIS markings and different directions among supplied studio/marine labels. Keep those discrepancies in internal review notes; publish no new numeric safety badge or dosage promise until the actual stocked label is reconciled.

## Claims to leave out of the renovated page

- **“Acid-free”** as an unqualified chemistry claim: use “made without hydrofluoric acid” or identify SynTech instead. The manufacturer's own materials use both acid-free marketing and synthetic-acid terminology.
- **“Safe on every aluminum finish,” “no PPE,” or “harmless to skin/eyes.”** The 2015 EMS SDS reports mild skin/eye irritation despite its HMIS 0/0/0 values and advises avoiding mist and skin/eye contact. [SDS](https://www.russtech.com/uploads/sds/Alumi-Brite-SDS.pdf)
- **“PFAS-free” or “fluorine-free.”** Reviewed formula literature identifies a fluorosurfactant; no current ingredient statement establishes either claim. Do not infer a regulatory PFAS classification from that term alone.
- **Current NSF, FDA, USDA, EPA approval, or Safer Choice certification badges** based only on technical-sheet wording or inherited logos.
- **Guaranteed cheaper shipping, no containment, zero worker risk, or unrestricted runoff disposal.** The SDS's transportation section says not dangerous goods; that is not a promise about every shipping fee or site practice. It expressly says to keep product out of storm drains. [SDS](https://www.russtech.com/uploads/sds/Alumi-Brite-SDS.pdf)
- **HF competitors' recipes, hot-surface guidance, aircraft approval, polishing claims, or dwell times** copied from unrelated Aluma-Brite/Alumi-Brite products.

The page can remain confident and useful: lead with natural brightness, aluminum-specific cleaning, fleet applications, finish care, and accessible manufacturer documentation.
