Research only. No publication, deployment, or product-document changes performed.

## Product identity and positioning

MultiWash's supplied TDS describes the same three-part system as **EMS Fortis**: SynTech, SynClean, and Purgo. The ingredient story and much of the technical sheet match. This is strong documentary support for using Fortis as the manufacturer reference, alongside the owner's direct-reseller confirmation. It is not SynClean HD, SynClean N, or a neutral-pH general cleaner.

Current EMS [Industrial Cleaning](https://www.enviromfg.com/industrial-cleaning) identifies Fortis for exterior soil, soot, rust and mold/mildew stains, plus bathrooms and locker rooms. Its [currently linked Fortis TDS](https://www.enviromfg.com/s/fortis-tds.pdf) describes a concentrated cleaner/deodorizer combining SynTech, SynClean, and Purgo; non-emulsifying soil removal; HMIS 0-0-0; no VOCs or phosphates; and non-DOT-regulated transport. The TDS is ©2022, still linked on the manufacturer's current page. Retrieval date does not make its underlying tests new. Its dilution section directs readers to the label.

Recommended page: **Clean and deodorize, inside and out.** Separate two clear application groups:

- **Building exteriors and hardscape:** siding, concrete, pavers, wood, roofing, exterior buildup, compatible pressure-wash and drone systems.
- **Facility wet zones:** restrooms, showers, tile, grout, waste areas, floors and mixed soil/odor cleanup.

Explain the combined cleaning/deodorizing job. Avoid medical-disinfection positioning, hand use, and cross-transferring Purgo's four-day persistence claim.

## Dilutions and method

The approved **general** label gives:

| Soil level | Printed dilution |
| --- | --- |
| Light soil / mild odor | 10:1 |
| Moderate soil / odor | 5:1 |
| Severe soil / odor | 2:1 |

Neither this artwork nor the older text label explicitly states the order of water and concentrate. Preserve the notation; do not invent RTU yield or per-gallon economics until the mixing convention is confirmed.

The older text label specifies foam cannon, mop or brush; work into the surface, allow 10–15 minutes, then wash away; heavily soiled areas or moss get 20 minutes. The gym brochure repeats 10–15 minutes for light/moderate wet-zone soil and up to 20 minutes for severe soil. The older text label also permits drying for severe odor problems; do not turn that exceptional instruction into a universal no-rinse claim. Main page workflow should emphasize application, agitation as needed, and rinsing.

## Technical facts and claim judgment

The supplied **MultiWash SDS**, rather than a family-wide inference, reports:

- **pH <0** in Section IX. The TDS supplies no exact pH. Current catalog `cat: "alkaline"` is not a sound chemical description of this product. Keep customer-facing wording “multi-surface cleaner and deodorizer”; do not call it neutral or alkaline.
- Skin irritation Category 3, eye irritation Category 2B, with mild skin and eye irritation statements. “Non-corrosive” is compatible with these statements; “cannot irritate skin/eyes” is not.
- US DOT non-regulated as a hazardous material. This supports non-hazmat product transport messaging, not a guaranteed all-in shipping-price comparison.
- Section VIII says eye/skin protection is not required for ordinary use, while allowing nuisance-level protection and naming nitrile gloves. Avoid extending that into a blanket occupational-risk guarantee or universal respirator-free fogging recommendation.
- Section XIII directs surplus/non-recyclable solution to a licensed disposal company and says waste generators must assess classification. Biodegradable product chemistry does not establish that dirty wash water carrying removed oils, soil, coatings or site contaminants may be discharged anywhere.

The supplied TDS supports no VOCs, no phosphates, water solubility, biodegradability, nonflammability, broad material compatibility and the cleaning mechanism. It describes non-emulsifying lifting of petroleum oils and animal/vegetable fats. Useful concise marketing: **“Lift mixed grime and tackle the odor in the same wash.”** Retain substrate spot-testing and normal rinse guidance in the application section, without turning the sales page into a warning document.

The TDS cites NSF categories and FDA/USDA language; regional distributors repeat these and display broad logo blocks. No exact current product registration number or EPA Safer Choice listing was located in the searches performed. Do not convert those repeated statements into a newly verified product certification. The source's FIFRA 25(b) exemption statement is not an EPA approval. Use the technical source link; keep unverified logo/approval claims out of the renovated hero.

Shelf-life conflict: supplied MultiWash TDS says 2 years; current EMS Fortis TDS says 1 year; EMS Cleaning distributor says over 3 years. Omit shelf-life marketing until the supplied batch/spec is resolved.
