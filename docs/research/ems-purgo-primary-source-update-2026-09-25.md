## Latest publicly linked standard-Purgo directions

The [manufacturer-linked label](https://www.purgotestresults.com/s/Purgo.pdf) requires pre-cleaning and describes spray, wipe, sponge, or mop application. It gives these instructions:

| Application | Label dilution / dose | Recommended contact time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Food-contact surface odor control | 1:32 | 1 minute |
| Non-food-contact surface odor control | 1:64 | 1 minute |
| Spoilage, deterioration, fouling on non-porous surfaces | 1:10 | 2 minutes |
| Odor-causing bacteria / odor removal | 1:64 | 1 minute |
| Mold-stain removal | 1:10 | 3 minutes |
| Microorganisms infectious only to animals | 1:32 | 2 minutes |
| Cooling-tower bacteria | 100 ppm | Not specified in this table |
| Algae | Initial 20 ppm; subsequent 5–10 ppm as needed | Not specified in this table |

These are source-document transcriptions, not a newly approved VertKleen use protocol. Confirm the ratio convention and whether ppm denotes product or active ingredient before operational dosing. Do not invent missing contact times.

A manufacturer-document conflict remains: the [TDS](https://www.enviromfg.com/s/Purgo-tds.pdf) lists 1:64 for animal-only microorganisms, whereas the label lists 1:32. Neither supplies the gym rendering's 1:16 high-touch instruction. The label does not supply the broad no-rinse, electrostatic, or occupied-space ULV protocol proposed in the original notes.

If EMS confirms a concentrate:water convention, 1:64 would yield 65 finished gallons per gallon of concentrate: $42.99 / 65 = approximately **$0.66 per finished gallon**. This is a conditional calculation, not a replacement for the approved application table or live selected-variant pricing.

## Fogging, residues, and four-day performance

Fogging is explicitly listed on the [EMS antimicrobial page](https://www.enviromfg.com/sanitizers), and the [vehicle-washing page](https://www.enviromfg.com/vehicle-washing) describes use on vehicle interior surfaces. This strengthens the basis for a manufacturer-directed fogging application section. It does not establish safe application around occupants, a particular ULV device, electrostatic compatibility, no respirator requirement, or unlimited surface compatibility.

The TDS reports no VOCs, non-regulated transport classifications, a one-year shelf life, and a 214°F boiling point. Those are manufacturer statements in the currently linked document, not independent testing performed here. The boiling point differs from the local Purgo SDS's CR-like 234°F value. A current exact-formula SDS is still required; the TDS is not a substitute. [EMS TDS](https://www.enviromfg.com/s/Purgo-tds.pdf).

The current upstream persistence PDF's SHA-256 is identical to the `source_sha256` in MASEST's [document ledger](../../data/public-document-review.json). Thus the earlier study critique concerns the current publicly linked study, not an accidentally stale local version. The same four-day hold, absent documented fresh-inoculation/wear challenges, and internal method/results inconsistencies remain. See the [earlier detailed review](../reviews/purgo-marine-recommendations-2026-09-25.md).

No reviewed first-party document establishes the proposed non-volatile protective-barrier explanation. The public ingredient label also does not establish the live-vegetative-microbe mechanism described in the local “Purgo Technology 101” document. Resolve that document's formula applicability instead of joining the two narratives.

## Patent findings and their limits

The current [EPA Purgo N label](https://www3.epa.gov/pesticides/chem_search/ppls/082859-00002-20250512.pdf) explicitly names **US10,194,657** and **US10,813,353**, providing an official label-to-patent connection for Purgo N. No corresponding public crosswalk was established for the standard citric-acid/lemongrass Purgo formula.

**These patents do contain residual-performance material.** Their dependent claims 4–5 recite at least 99.99% effectiveness against *S. aureus* up to five and twelve days after application. Examples describe dried treated plates challenged afterward; that is more directly relevant to residual activity than the standard-Purgo four-day-hold report. However, this is a different formulation family, does not establish field durability under wear/washing, and does not add a residual claim to the current EPA label. [US10194657B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10194657B2/en), [US10813353B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10813353B2/en).

See [patent-record appendix](ems-patent-records-2026-09-25.md) for those patents and the wider EMS-related portfolio, including dates, assignee/license evidence, claims, and official-source links. EMS's public patent page is a request form. A patent about acid replacement or a different antimicrobial formula cannot establish standard Purgo's composition or 96-hour field performance. Patent publication, granted claims, current legal status, product identity, and regulatory approval are separate facts.
