# VertKleen HVAC CR: manufacturer research and page decisions

Research date: September 25, 2026. Product: VertKleen HVAC CR, SKU VK-CR2. Research supports the product-page renovation; no deployment or external submissions.

## Positioning and identity

Lead with **grease and organic buildup in drains, pans and service areas**, the uses named on the exact HVAC CR label. Present the 60% caustic-replacement reference as product strength, separate from the dilution schedule. The manufacturer's [pHlexaPeel products page](https://www.phlexapeel.com/products) explicitly identifies CR2 as a heavy-grease/carbon cleaner with strength equivalent to 60% sodium hydroxide. Its separate CR water-treatment entry also says 60%; that inconsistent family naming does not override the explicit CR2 entry or exact SKU identity.

The [EMS manufacturer catalog](https://www.enviromfg.com/products1) lists pHlex CR and CR2 among its specialty/private-label formulations. Its [technology page](https://www.enviromfg.com/our-juice2) describes SynClean as the high-pH replacement platform used for cleaning, water treatment, drain cleaning, degreasing and carbon removal. This supports the technology context, not a transfer of every SynClean HD/LF, Elevate or CIP CR specification to HVAC CR.

The exact MASEST label says **caustic replacement · drain & degreaser**. Keep that identity central. A pH-adjustment application can be offered through technical support; a universal water-treatment dose is not established by the HVAC cleaning label.

## Controlling local label: directions and compatibility

Source: `docs/labels/hvac/vertkleen-hvac-cr-label-6x8.pdf`. This is an image-only PDF, rendered and visually inspected during this review.

| Job | Label dilution | Label dwell and finish |
|---|---|---|
| Light soil / mild odor | 10:1 | Apply, dwell 10–15 minutes, rinse. |
| Moderate soil / odor | 5:1 | Dwell, then rinse; no minute count provided. |
| Severe soil / odor | 2:1 | Dwell 20 minutes, rinse. |

The label does not explicitly name the ratio order. Preserve the printed ratios rather than silently adding water:product assumptions, exact RTU yields or cost-per-diluted-gallon calculations. It does not prescribe a fixed ounce-per-drain dose, continuous-feed rate, blocked-drain recovery time or universal dwell for the moderate row.

The label states non-corrosive caustic replacement, grease/organic-buildup removal, biodegradation in less than ten days, triple-zero HMIS, and aluminum compatibility **when diluted**. Preserve that dilution condition. It does not establish compatibility with every coil coating, seal, alloy or electrical component, nor justify presenting this as an all-purpose no-rinse coil spray.

For applications, a practical sequence is accessible soil removal, label-matched dilution, dwell, water rinse and a flow check. Do not invent a treatment protocol for inaccessible or completely blocked drains. Do not turn odor/organic-soil cleaning into a disinfection claim.

## Manufacturer label and useful technical facts

The current [pHlex CR2 manufacturer label](https://www.phlexapeel.com/s/pHlex-CR2-Label.pdf), linked directly from the manufacturer's product page, identifies CR2 as a caustic replacement for water treatment and food processing. It lists non-DOT-regulated status under 49 CFR 173.154, GRAS and Kosher-PAREVE classifications. Its directions refer to a technical data sheet. It also includes mild skin/eye irritation statements and disposal according to applicable requirements. Therefore non-hazmat shipping is supported; guaranteed freight-price reductions, zero possible exposure injury and unrestricted water/ground disposal are not established by this label.

Source: `docs/sds/watersafe60-cr-nsf60-user-guide.pdf`, manufacturer guide version 1.4, revised February 4, 2026; website distribution cover is a separate date. The guide explicitly distinguishes pHlex CR at 40% caustic replacement and CR² at 60%. It states that **either CR product** can be diluted with water without a reaction, and its last page explicitly identifies **CR2 pH above 13**. These are appropriate CR2 technical-profile points alongside the exact HVAC label's HMIS and biodegradation claims.

The guide's preceding paragraphs describe pHlex CR as non-fuming, with no traditional hydroxides, no salt residue and water-rinse neutralization. Those paragraphs name CR, not every CR2 variant. Avoid presenting them as newly verified, exact HVAC CR formulation specifications unless the current CR2 TDS supplies the same statements. The guide contains **no aluminum restriction**. Older VertKleen CR/CIP transfer-fitting warnings concern different documentation; they should not silently override the exact HVAC label or become blanket CR2 incompatibility claims.

## Certification scope

The [official NSF EMS listing](https://info.nsf.org/Certified/PwsChemicals/Listings.asp?Company=C0284921&Standard=060), checked September 25, 2026, lists **pHlex CR**, maximum 92 mg/L, for pH adjustment, precipitation and softening. It does **not** list pHlex CR2 or VertKleen HVAC CR by those names. Do not attach the 92 mg/L potable limit or this named certification to the HVAC product. The current CR2 manufacturer label's classification block does not include NSF60.

Food-process classifications are supporting chemistry context, not the central conversion story for an HVAC drain cleaner. No CR2-specific current Safer Choice directory entry or underlying NSF nonfood certificate was established in this review. Manufacturer/distributor criteria-conformity language should not be transformed into a newly verified certification badge.

## Patent and test context

The manufacturer markets CR2 as patented on its products page. The [EMS patent page](https://www.enviromfg.com/patents) remains a request form and provides no public formula-to-patent crosswalk. No request submitted. The earlier [CIP CR research note](cip-cr-external-research-2026-09-25.md) records the official USPTO examination of US 11,311,036 B2 and related synthetic-base patents; its issued claims concern hydraulic fracturing and do not independently identify the current HVAC CR SKU. Avoid adding a specific patent number as product-performance proof.

No exact HVAC CR drain field trial, numerical head-to-head cleaning study or current manufacturer-issued full CR2 toxicology report was established in this review. Do not relabel the brewery CR/HCR trial, the Carib CR test or SynClean HD aircraft test as HVAC CR results. The exact label, explicit manufacturer CR2 identity and practical instructions provide a stronger page than unrelated proof cards.

## Recommended page content

- Lead: concentrated drain and degreasing cleaner for grease and organic buildup.
- Show the three exact label dilutions with their actual dwell instructions.
- Technical profile: pH above 13, HMIS 0-0-0, less-than-ten-day biodegradation per local label, water dilution without an exothermic reaction per the CR/CR2 guide, and manufacturer non-DOT-regulated transport classification.
- Use a distinct 60% caustic-replacement reference with a link to the manufacturer's CR2 product/label information; no dose-equivalence shortcut.
- Link the current product label and technical-support request; offer pH-treatment planning separately.
- Keep diluted-aluminum wording qualified. Screen existing application imagery for synthetic components and record replacement needs; no new images generated by this research task.

All conflicting-document detail above is internal review context. Customer copy can remain concise, assured and task-focused.
