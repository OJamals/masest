# Purgo distributor insights: additional sources

Reviewed September 25, 2026. Internal research supporting original MASEST copy. Distributor pages show useful positioning and buying patterns; the MASEST product label remains the source for its directions.

## Strongest points to integrate

1. **Lead with odor control in busy spaces.** Make the product useful immediately: locker rooms, restrooms, shared equipment surfaces, waste containers, and vehicle interiors. Show recognizable jobs before chemistry.
2. **Give fleet interiors their own application card.** This reaches commercial buyers beyond marine and water treatment. Keep surface cleaning and odor control the stated purpose.
3. **Show concentrate value beside the price.** Use the application-specific label dilution. At the supplied $42.99 price, one gallon plus 64 gallons of water yields 65 gallons at about $0.66 per mixed gallon; one plus 32 yields 33 gallons at about $1.30. These calculations assume ratios mean product:water. Use the current storefront price in implementation.
4. **Make ordering scale obvious.** Display available retail and bulk sizes together. Offer product guidance and bulk quotes using MASEST's actual fulfillment options.
5. **Put product documents beside the buying action.** Label, technical data, and SDS links should be easy to find. An application question should lead directly to an answer or a quote.

## Sources and useful patterns

### ZEL Distribution, Southern Ontario

[Transit and fleet page](https://zeldistro.com/transit) assigns each EMS product a clear role: Torque for exterior washing, Purgo for interior odor control, FogX for storage protection. Its Purgo section specifies bus, coach, cruiser, and ambulance cabins, emphasizes odor-causing bacteria, and advertises dilution up to 64:1. Sample requests, technical data, FAQs, and direct contacts sit near the product story.

**Borrow:** a fleet-interior use card, concentrate economics, accessible documents, and a clear bulk-buying action. Proposed MASEST wording: **“Freshen the spaces your fleet works in. Control odor-causing bacteria on cabin surfaces with a concentrate built for routine interior care.”**

Keep its adjacent OEM approvals attached to Torque and its storage-protection duration attached to FogX. A free-sample promise should appear only if MASEST actually offers one. This page supplies no independent Purgo efficacy study.

### Cleanse It All / Cleanse It All Global

[Purgo product page](https://cleanseitall.com/purgo/) gives concrete locations and surfaces, including gyms, locker rooms, restaurants, public transport, counters, floors, sinks, showers, handles, and waste containers. It groups description, technical data, and SDS, then offers a packaging ladder from spray bottles to totes.

**Borrow:** replace vague “wet areas” copy with specific tasks, then connect small orders to larger replenishment needs. Proposed heading: **“One concentrate. More places to freshen.”**

The page contains duplicated product blocks, inconsistent SKU/price entries, and directions beginning at 50% dilution followed by rinsing. Do not reuse those prices or instructions for the MASEST SKU. Its [company benefits page](https://cleanseitall.com/benefits/) identifies the business as a direct EMS distributor and presents operational savings as a buying theme. Borrow concentrate value and simpler product selection; do not turn broad predictions about injuries, fines, or insurance costs into product promises.

### EMS Russia

[Purgo product page](https://ems-rus.com/projects/purgo/) combines room cleaning, odor control, a visible package-size selector, application illustrations, before/after images, FAQs, and a presentation/quote request. It also names cooling towers, algae, and mold stains separately from everyday surfaces.

**Borrow:** organize applications by customer job, with distinct surface and water-system sections. Make package selection and a bulk quote easy. Original heading: **“Built for everyday surfaces. Ready for specialized maintenance.”**

Its regional copy describes live microorganisms plus SynTech. Its FAQ limits use to hard surfaces while another section references textiles. These inconsistencies make it a positioning reference, not a new formula or material-compatibility source. Do not copy its temperature, microorganism-count, or skin-safety claims into the MASEST page.

### Ukrainian distributor-hosted EMS literature

[Avto-Sintez Purgo brochure](https://avto-sintez.com.ua/pdf/002_purgo-product-description-en.pdf) carries EMS branding and a 2019 copyright. It describes surface cleaning, odor control, grease/organic-soil applications, algae, and cooling-tower fouling. Dilution instructions point readers to the product label. This is historical EMS literature hosted by a distributor, not a newly published 2026 study.

**Borrow:** the breadth of everyday surface applications and a clear path to application-specific directions. Its layout also supports separating practical benefits from technical reference material. Do not import its live-microbe mechanism as a verified description of the current MASEST formula.

### EcoTiv Clean

Searches under EcoTivClean, EcoTiv Clean, EMS Israel, and Hebrew variants did not resolve a verifiable first-party product page in this pass. Earlier supplied material and the persistence-study client name remain the available references. No new live distributor claims were added from this lead.

## Original copy candidates

**Hero:**

> Fresh spaces start with Purgo.
>
> Concentrated odor control for the places people share. Put Purgo to work on hard surfaces throughout locker rooms, restrooms, facilities, and fleet interiors.

**Application cards:**

- **Facilities and shared spaces:** Freshen counters, handles, showers, floors, and waste containers with application-specific dilution and contact times.
- **Fleet interiors:** Make cabin surface care part of the regular cleaning routine, with economical concentrate for repeated use across the fleet.
- **Marine and water systems:** Target specialized maintenance tasks with their own dosing instructions, separate from everyday surface cleaning.

**Value block:**

> Small amount. Long way to go.
>
> Mix for the job and see the cost per ready-to-use gallon before you buy.

**Buying action:**

> Choose your size. Plan your supply.
>
> Order for everyday use or request a quote for a larger operation.

These are original formulations of shared marketing themes, not copied distributor prose. Customer-facing copy needs no cancellation/non-launch narrative. Keep product identity and source checks in internal research.
