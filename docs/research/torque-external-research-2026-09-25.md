# Torque: external research and page direction

Research date: 2026-09-25. Product: EMS Torque Super Duty Truck Wash and Wax, sold by MASEST as the same manufacturer chemistry. This note supports the product renovation; it does not publish changes or replace the supplied product label.

## Recommended direction

Lead with **fleet wash, wax, and easier repeat cleaning**. Give buyers concrete jobs: diesel soot, road grime, insect residue, and winter road treatments. Follow with finish compatibility, OEM testing, and clear mixing/application instructions. This is a complete exterior fleet-care product, with a stronger story than generic “truck wash.”

The clearest independently retrievable manufacturer connection is Blue Bird: EMS currently identifies Torque as the chemistry behind Bird Bath Bus Wash and states that PPG tested its compatibility with Blue Bird coatings. Use that specific relationship as evidence, rather than an unexplained wall of OEM logos. [EMS vehicle washing](https://www.enviromfg.com/vehicle-washing)

The implementation pass visually reviewed MASEST's published general label and confirmed **20:1 new/reconditioned, 15:1 moderate, 10:1 heavy, and 5:1 severe**. The label does not state which side is water or concentrate. Display these exact label ratios without inventing their order or a ready-to-use yield. This local label takes precedence over foreign distributor directions. Neither current manufacturer document reviewed establishes cost per truck or square-foot coverage.

The implementation pass also identified the approved `yellowfin-torque-wash` proof entry in [the local proof catalog](../../data/content/proof.json). Use that Torque-specific wash evidence instead of the combined airboat material. This selection and the label observations were supplied by the parent implementation review; the external sources above were retrieved directly during this research pass.

## Registry and patent checks

**EPA Safer Choice:** retrieved the complete [EPA Envirofacts dataset](https://data.epa.gov/efservice/t_safer_choice_and_design_for_the_environment/JSON), linked by [EPA's dataset page](https://www.epa.gov/enviro/download-additional-envirofacts-datasets), on 2026-09-25. It contained 4,993 rows. Case-insensitive searches for `Torque`, `Environmental Manufacturing`, `Bird Bath`, `enviromfg`, `SynTech`, and `SynClean` returned no rows. This does not establish the absence of every private-label entry or historical approval; it does not establish a current Torque certification either. Keep manufacturer criteria-conformity wording separate from an independently verified certification badge. EPA also explicitly encourages limiting direct environmental releases, including for products marketed for outdoor use. [EPA product search](https://www.epa.gov/saferchoice/products)

**NSF:** performed the actual POST searches at the [NSF White Book form](https://info.nsf.org/USDA/psnclistings.asp), with results dated September 25, 2026. `CompanyName=Environmental Manufacturing` returned Elevate 148037 (A1) and Renovo HD 148038 (A1/K2), two products. `ProductName=Torque` returned 25 lubricant entries from five unrelated manufacturers; no EMS Torque. The TDS's NSF A1/G1 entry is therefore manufacturer collateral that this current directory check did not independently corroborate. GET requests to the results URL ignore these form fields and produce unfiltered results; they were not treated as evidence.

**Patents:** EMS's [technology page](https://www.enviromfg.com/our-juice2) presents SynTech as acid-replacement chemistry and SynClean as caustic-replacement chemistry; its [patent information page](https://www.enviromfg.com/patents) is a request form. The patent text for [US11034598B2, Synthetic acid and associated methods](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11034598B2/en) identifies Green Products and Technologies as assignee and John MacDonald inventors. This corroborates the underlying technology portfolio; it does not identify the complete current Torque formulation or certify product performance. No public product-to-patent-number crosswalk specifically for Torque was located. The prior repository [EMS patent review](ems-patent-records-2026-09-25.md) provides the broader portfolio and official USPTO proceeding evidence of EMS licensing. No request submitted or new ownership/status opinion made.

**Name collisions:** Torque Detail automotive soaps, TWS Torque/Impact two-step bundles, and EPA Torque fungicides are different products. Their instructions, registrations and comparisons must not be imported into this EMS product page. [Torque Detail](https://www.torquedetail.com/products/decon-wash-soap-decontamination-soap-8-oz), [TWS product list](https://truckwashsoap.com/products/), [unrelated EPA Torque fungicide label](https://www3.epa.gov/pesticides/chem_search/ppls/001001-00087-20131115.pdf)

## Implementation judgment

Use assertive product copy around cleaning results, wax, finish compatibility, easier repeat washing, non-DOT shipping classification, and the specific Blue Bird/PPG evidence. Label comparisons by the conventional chemistry being compared. Use a fleet-specific sample CTA and a drum/tote wash-program quote CTA. Place the manufacturer TDS and supplied SDS next to the application information.

The user's reseller relationship authorizes using manufacturer materials; it is not a reason to swap product identities or broaden a technical claim beyond its source. Keep unresolved badge, dilution, warranty, freight-price, and wastewater assertions in this research note rather than filling the customer page with disclaimers. A biodegradable cleaner and wash water containing removed diesel residue, metals or road contaminants are different materials; no source reviewed supports unrestricted dumping of the latter.
