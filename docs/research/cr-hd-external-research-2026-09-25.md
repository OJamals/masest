# VertKleen CR HD: product evidence and renovation recommendations

Research date: September 25, 2026. Product: VertKleen CR HD (`crhd`). Current EMS pages and PDFs, official NSF records, current competitor documentation, distributor materials, and supplied product documents were reviewed. Research only; no publication or external submissions.

## Recommended product story

Lead with **high-foam grease removal for kitchens, equipment, and facility cleaning**. Explain that HD means high detergency. CR HD lifts petroleum oils, animal and vegetable fats, protein residue, and grime; the product's non-emulsifying action keeps released grease intact for removal and separation. Feature the genuine Fort Lauderdale kitchen before/after, show the current label's four practical dilutions, and make the Low Foam alternative easy to find for automatic scrubbers and recirculating equipment.

The strongest evidence is the supplied CR HD TDS plus EMS's current SynClean HD page. The local TDS closely follows EMS's SynClean HD document, including high foam, non-butyl/non-solvent chemistry, soil types, and application examples. This supports the product-family relationship under the user's direct-reseller confirmation. It does not establish a Renovo HD registration-number crosswalk. Sources: [supplied CR HD TDS](/Users/omar/Desktop/masest/sds/VertKleen%20CRHD%20TDS.pdf), [EMS products](https://www.enviromfg.com/products1).

## Manufacturer evidence

EMS currently positions SynClean HD as its foaming industrial degreaser, distinct from LF and neutral-pH versions. Its published TDS supports solvent- and butyl-free grease lifting, high detergency, cold/hot water use, HMIS 0-0-0, zero VOCs, non-flammability, and non-regulated transport. The sheet identifies non-emulsifying action and directs readers to the label for dilution specifications. It reports non-skin-irritant, non-mutagenic and biodegradability classifications, but provides summaries rather than underlying laboratory reports. [Current EMS SynClean HD TDS](https://www.enviromfg.com/s/syncleanhdwso-tds.pdf)

The supplied CR HD TDS gives the same practical benefits, including rust/corrosion inhibitors. Use clear advantages such as “Lift grease without solvents or butyls,” “High foam for hands-on cleaning,” and “One concentrate for equipment, kitchens, and facility surfaces.” Do not turn a manufacturer classification into a promise of zero injury, zero worker compensation exposure, or universal compatibility under every use condition. [Supplied CR HD TDS](/Users/omar/Desktop/masest/sds/VertKleen%20CRHD%20TDS.pdf)

The current EMS technology page identifies SynClean as the high-pH caustic/solvent replacement platform. Its patent link still opens a request form, not a numbered product-to-patent list. Existing portfolio research identifies related synthetic-base patents, but no exact CR HD formulation-to-grant mapping was established here. “EMS SynClean technology” is stronger than adding an unverified patent number. [EMS technology](https://www.enviromfg.com/our-juice2), [EMS patent request](https://www.enviromfg.com/patents), [earlier patent dossier](ems-patent-records-2026-09-25.md)

## Label directions and dilution priority

The text-based supplied CR HD label specifies:

| Task | Label dilution |
|---|---|
| Floors | 40:1 |
| General cleaning | 30:1 |
| Degreasing | 10:1 |
| Heavy degreasing | 3:1 |

Application: spray, sponge, brush, or mop; brush and rinse with fresh water; repeat as needed. The table does not explicitly define ratio order. Preserve its notation unless current packaging supplies the missing definition. No universal contact time is provided. Source: [CR HD label](/Users/omar/Desktop/masest/sds/VertKleen%20CRHD%20Label.pdf).

The supplied TDS gives separate examples: 5:1 hydraulic fluid, 10:1 hoods/milk residue, 30:1 carpets/vehicles/floors, 100:1 windows. These are application examples, not a reason to replace the label's task table. Its instruction is to refer to the label. A 2024 distributor TDS corroborates the label's 40:1 / 30:1 / 10:1 / 3:1 table and wet-surface application followed by agitation and rinsing. [Supplied CR HD TDS](/Users/omar/Desktop/masest/sds/VertKleen%20CRHD%20TDS.pdf), [Trident SynClean HD TDS](https://tridentcleanpro.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/SYNCLEAN-HD-TDS-7-29.pdf)

The image-only updated 6×8 label requires visual review; text extraction returns no directions. Keep supplied documents intact and resolve version differences in the review record. [Updated CR HD label](/Users/omar/Desktop/masest/updates/VertKleen_CRHD_Label_6x8.pdf)

It does not document elapsed cleaning time, dilution, labor savings, or measured percentage removal. Do not add those numbers. ## Comparison data: historical sheet needs correction

The supplied “Water Base Degreasers Comparison” is a formulation/characteristics table, not a controlled cleaning trial. It gives CR HD 30% active chemicals and 70% water; the distribution-center account instead says 50% degreaser. Neither supports a cleaning-speed multiplier, a 2× efficacy claim, or a current competitor formulation claim. The sheet also lacks an identified test laboratory, method, date, replicate count and measured soil-removal endpoint. [Supplied comparison](/Users/omar/Desktop/masest/sds/VertKleen%20CRHD%20Comparision%20with%20other%20Degreasers.pdf)

Its blanket Simple Green butoxyethanol statement is contradicted by the current US manufacturer's product page, which expressly describes the industrial cleaner as butyl-, solvent- and phosphate-free. That page also identifies Safer Choice certification. [Simple Green industrial product](https://simplegreen.com/industrial/products/industrial-cleaner-degreaser/)

The actual current SDS at the stable download URL is **13008-25A, October 1, 2025**, superseding March 12, 2024; search snippets still displayed the earlier revision. It identifies a formulation change, lists water above 85%, omits 2-butoxyethanol, gives pH 8–10, classifies the product as non-hazardous under OSHA HCS, and describes non-regulated shipping. It reports 80% biodegradation in 28 days under OECD 301D. Thus the historical comparison's “unknown” biodegradation time and implied toxicity contrast are unsuitable as current facts. [Current Simple Green US industrial SDS](https://cdn.simplegreen.com/downloads/SDS_EN-US_SimpleGreenIndustrialCleanerDegreaser.pdf)

Recommendation: remove the historical comparison download from this renovated product page while preserving the controlled file and global registry. Direct buyers to an application-matched comparison and current technical-data request. Compare dilution, working time, agitation, rinse water, repeat passes and total job cost. No reliable numeric CR HD-versus-current-Simple-Green cleaning test was found in the reviewed material.

### Linked comparison-page review

The existing `/comparisons/cr-hd-vs-simple-green` page also needs correction before the renovated product page recommends it. The reviewed HTML says CR HD is 50% degreaser and Simple Green 15% active, then treats that arithmetic as proof of near-neat competitor usage and superior cleaning capacity. This repeats the inconsistent field-report number and infers performance without a comparative test. Replace that passage with dilution, repeat-pass and finished-job measurements.

The page's introduction and table also limit Simple Green's published directions to full strength through 1:10. Its current official directions actually give heavy cleaning from full strength to 10 parts water, medium cleaning 10–30 parts water, light cleaning 30+ parts water, and mechanically assisted cleaning 20–30 parts water. Correct that range. [Current Simple Green directions](https://simplegreen.com/industrial/products/industrial-cleaner-degreaser/)

These findings were sent to the parent implementation agent; this research task did not edit comparison code or generated HTML.

## Certifications and handling

The **official NSF listing current September 25, 2026** lists EMS **Renovo HD, registration 148038, A1 and K2**. It does not list SynClean HD or VertKleen CR HD under those names. The official letter specifies a potable-water rinse for A1 general cleaning around food-processing areas. The Renovo name must not silently become a CR HD registration badge. [Live NSF EMS listing](https://info.nsf.org/USDA/Listings.asp?Company=C0146843), [NSF Renovo HD letter](https://info.nsf.org/USDA/letters/148038.pdf)

EMS's current SynClean TDS itself gives inconsistent NSF category sets across its two pages. Its Safer Choice statement describes meeting program requirements; it is not an exact current certified-SKU directory entry. No exact SynClean HD or Renovo HD EPA Safer Choice entry was established in this bounded search. Recommend presenting verified product properties and linking the manufacturer TDS rather than expanding the badge list. [EMS SynClean TDS](https://www.enviromfg.com/s/syncleanhdwso-tds.pdf)

The supplied SDS records pH 13.5–14 and HMIS 0-0-0. Its routine-use protection language says normal ventilation is generally adequate and optional protection may be used; its label still carries eye/skin irritation cautions. The SDS explicitly says to prevent entry to storm drains and directs surplus/non-recyclable solution to a licensed disposal company. Biodegradability therefore cannot be rewritten as unrestricted release of product or oily rinsate to land or water. For the page, a practical workflow can simply direct crews to collect the loosened grease and rinse water. [CR HD SDS](/Users/omar/Desktop/masest/sds/VertKleen%20CRHD%20SDS.pdf), [CR HD label](/Users/omar/Desktop/masest/sds/VertKleen%20CRHD%20Label.pdf)

## Distributor ideas worth adapting

- **1st Ayd:** clearly calls SynClean HD high-foaming and describes a broad range of soil types and work methods. Useful merchandising: one concentrate across equipment, kitchens and facility cleaning, with foam choice made explicit. Corroborated by EMS and the local CR HD TDS. [1st Ayd product](https://www.1stayd.com/synclean-hd-high-foaming-cleaner-degreaser)
- **Trident:** practical four-row dilution table and apply/agitate/rinse sequence match the supplied product label. Use that clarity; do not transfer every certification string. [Trident TDS](https://tridentcleanpro.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/SYNCLEAN-HD-TDS-7-29.pdf)
- **EMS Cleaning Spain:** useful full-job comparison dimensions and application-specific cleaning advice. Its Catalonia example is explicitly simulated and gives a six-month ROI estimate; exclude that case, its ROI and illustrative imagery from real-results content. [EMS Cleaning guide](https://www.emscleaning.eu/en/sustituto-sosa-caustica-synclean-hd/)
- **Overgreen:** useful segmentation across restaurants, transport and industrial facilities. Its disinfection/antimicrobial language is not supported by the reviewed CR HD label and should not transfer to this degreaser page. [Overgreen SynClean HD](https://www.overgreenperu.com/synclean-hd/)

## Implementation priorities

1. Replace generic industrial-degreaser positioning with high-foam grease/fat removal and the real kitchen result.
2. Correct “keeps grease suspended” language to lifting and removing intact grease, consistent with the non-emulsifying product story.
3. Publish the exact label table and short apply/brush/rinse workflow; keep TDS examples distinct.
4. Link Low Foam prominently for scrubbers and circulation equipment.
5. Highlight HMIS 0-0-0, zero VOCs and non-regulated shipping without speculative cost guarantees.
6. Remove the outdated comparison PDF from this page's recommended documents; retain source files and internal evidence of the discrepancy.
7. Keep customer proof, product specifications and certification identity separate in the research record, while the shopper-facing copy stays direct and useful.
