## File findings

### MASEST Purgo Label.pdf and Purgo1.pdf

[MASEST label](</Users/omar/Desktop/masest/purgo/MASEST Purgo Label.pdf>) is the existing private-label source. [Purgo1.pdf](/Users/omar/Desktop/masest/purgo/Purgo1.pdf) is byte-identical to the [current public original-Purgo label](https://www.purgotestresults.com/s/Purgo.pdf). Extracted text differs only in the supplier block: EMS in Melbourne becomes MASEST-VertKleen in Cocoa. Visual review confirms MASEST/VertKleen branding and a 0-0-0 hazard diamond.

Both list citric acid **0.60%**, lemongrass oil **0.15%**, other ingredients **99.25%**; the latter are water, soap bark (Quillaja saponin), and stearic acid. Both require pre-cleaning and list spray, wipe, sponge or mop application. Directions:

| Application | Stated dilution/dose | Stated contact time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Food-contact surface odor control | 1:32 | 1 minute |
| Non-food-contact surface odor control | 1:64 | 1 minute |
| Spoilage, deterioration, fouling on nonporous surfaces | 1:10 | 2 minutes |
| Bacteria in cooling towers | 100 ppm (100 mg/L) | Not stated alongside this dose |
| Algae growth | Initial 20 ppm; subsequent 5–10 ppm as needed | Not stated |
| Odor neutralization/removal | 1:64 | 1 minute |
| Mold-stain removal | 1:10 | 3 minutes |
| Microorganisms infectious only to animals | 1:32 | 2 minutes |

Neither supplies a 1:16 high-touch direction, skin-use instruction, ULV/electrostatic protocol, or explicit blanket no-rinse instruction. PDF modification metadata: MASEST copy March 13, 2026; EMS copy March 20, 2023. These are metadata dates, not declared formulation revisions.

### MASEST Purgo Base Data.pdf

[Three-page table](</Users/omar/Desktop/masest/purgo/MASEST Purgo Base Data.pdf>), already represented in the repository source ledger. Heading: **Purgo Virucidal Data. 06/2025**. Scope explicitly states a **Purgo raw material Base**, **5% serum**, **400 ppm hard water**, **10-minute contact time**, hard nonporous environmental surfaces. It cites EPA guidance DIS/TSS-7 and three lots/four-log reduction for Canada. These are table statements, not an official registration decision.

No laboratory identity, signed original report, exact final formulation, dilution, or commercial lot crosswalk appears. Virus rows do not establish performance of the sold diluted SKU, skin use, or residual activity. PDF metadata preserves title `Xtreme Base Data.xls`, creation April 21, 2009, modification May 6, 2026. The apparent date layering warrants provenance clarification; it does not itself prove fabrication or a new 2026 test.

### Purgo Bacterial Persist Test.pdf

[Five-page file](</Users/omar/Desktop/masest/purgo/Purgo Bacterial Persist Test.pdf>) is byte-identical to the [public study](https://www.purgotestresults.com/s/Purgo-Bacterial-Persist.pdf). All four scanned laboratory pages were visually reviewed. Report **20055117**, Milouda & Migal / Merieux NutriSciences, to ECOTIV CLEAN, dated **June 28, 2020**; sample received June 1, tested June 3, 2020.

The same limitations remain: inoculation precedes treatment; surfaces are held four days before testing; no documented fresh contamination or abrasion challenge establishes continuing protection against later contamination. The results table instead labels its endpoint after disinfection **30 seconds**. The conclusion's claimed two-log reduction for E. faecalis and E. aerogenes conflicts with reported counts, including E. faecalis 13,200 to 8,600 and 12,800 to 9,400. No exact tested formula/dilution is identified. The manufacturer's four-day summary is broader than the documented experiment. See the [earlier detailed critique](/Users/omar/Claude/Projects/MASEST/docs/reviews/purgo-marine-recommendations-2026-09-25.md).

### Purgo 101.pdf

[One-page technology description](</Users/omar/Desktop/masest/purgo/Purgo 101.pdf>), already the repository source. It describes stabilized live vegetative microbes, immediate metabolism, lipase production, organic-waste degradation, and stability up to 10:1 dilutions. It also calls the technology Xtreme. No strains, quantities, test identity, or mapping to the citric-acid/lemongrass label is supplied. This is not proof of a nonvolatile protective barrier. Metadata: 2009 creation; March 11, 2026 modification; legacy title `Microsoft Word - Xtreme Mic Technology 101.doc`.

### PurgoN+Label.pdf

[Two scanned label pages](/Users/omar/Desktop/masest/purgo/PurgoN+Label.pdf), visually reviewed, byte-identical to the [public Purgo N asset](https://www.purgotestresults.com/s/PurgoN-Label.pdf). Both PDF timestamps: November 7, 2024. It identifies **1,2-hexanediol 4.25%**, **EPA Reg. 82859-2**, non-food-contact hard/nonporous surfaces, 10-minute disinfection and 5-minute sanitization. Spray from 6–8 inches, maintain required wet contact time, then air-dry. It shows a 0-0-0 hazard diamond. No residual duration appears. It is a separate formulation from original Purgo. The [May 12, 2025 EPA label](https://www3.epa.gov/pesticides/chem_search/ppls/082859-00002-20250512.pdf) remains the newest accepted file returned by EPA's current API.

### Purgo.pdf

[Two-page Cleanse It All Global brochure](/Users/omar/Desktop/masest/purgo/Purgo.pdf), PDF metadata August 10, 2020. Its website footer is `www.cleanseitall.com`; it identifies the company as an EMS direct distributor. No original study reports or precise formulation accompany its broad marketing claims. Directions say most sizes are concentrated, wipes/disc dispensers pre-diluted to 50%, begin with 50% dilution and adjust for contamination, then apply, wait, and rinse. It reports no VOCs, a one-year shelf life and 214°F boiling point. This is historical distributor evidence, not the latest authoritative instructions; it is distinct from the current public one-page file also named `Purgo.pdf`.
