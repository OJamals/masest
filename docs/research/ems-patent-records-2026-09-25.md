# EMS patent research: Purgo linkage and latest located records

Research date: 2026-09-25. Scope: Environmental Manufacturing Solutions, LLC (EMS), its related patent holder Green Products & Technologies, LLC, and evidence relevant to Purgo surface cleaner. Public-source research; no manufacturer contact or site changes.

## Finding

An official product-to-patent link **does exist for Purgo N**: its EPA-hosted label dated **2025-05-12** names **US10194657** and **US10813353**. These antimicrobial patents contain multi-day efficacy claims and laboratory persistence examples. They belong to a different patent-holder lineage from the EMS/Green Products acid/base portfolio below.

This search did **not** establish that those patents cover **standard Purgo**, the citric-acid/lemongrass formulation. Neither a standard-Purgo 96-hour claim nor the proposed non-volatile, bio-based protective-barrier explanation follows from Purgo N's patents. This is a public-evidence gap, not proof that no standard-Purgo patent exists.

The newest located application publication in the related portfolio is **US20240327654A1**, published **2024-10-03**, for a hydrophobic protective coating. Its retrieved record includes prosecution activity dated **2026-05-14**. Neither that coating nor the synthetic acid/base patents below should be presented as Purgo's mechanism without an explicit manufacturer crosswalk.

## Company and patent-holder relationship

John MacDonald's declaration filed in **IPR2021-00357**, paragraphs 1–2, states that he owns EMS and Green Products, serves as Green Products' president, and that EMS is the exclusive licensee of US7938912 and several other Green Products patents. This is direct evidence in an official USPTO proceeding. It establishes the relationship at the time of the declaration, not a current assignment audit. [USPTO declaration](https://ptacts.uspto.gov/ptacts/public-informations/petitions/1545002/download-documents?artifactId=1c0A89wDfQmWiK5exdaOPvUFFjezGnW9HT4zBgjQcA2h9uEreFk0pCE)

EMS's current technology page separates SynTech (acid replacement), SynClean (base replacement), Purgo (surface cleaner/bleach replacement), Purgo AF (hand sanitizer), and Fusion (anti-stick treatment). Its patent link resolves to a **Patent Information Request Form**, not a public list mapping products to patents. `/patents1` returned 404. [EMS technology](https://www.enviromfg.com/our-juice2), [EMS patent request form](https://www.enviromfg.com/patents)

## Relevant families and records

### 0. Purgo N: officially identified antimicrobial patent family

**Product crosswalk:** The EPA-hosted **2025-05-12 Purgo N label**, PDF page 6, identifies U.S. Patents **10,194,657** and **10,813,353** alongside Zsolt's Formula and The Gilla Company. Page 3 specifies **4.25% 1,2-hexanediol**, with 95.75% other ingredients, for hard, non-porous, non-food-contact surfaces. This is the separate ready-to-use Purgo N product, not evidence for transferring claims to standard Purgo or its dilution instructions. [EPA Purgo N label](https://www3.epa.gov/pesticides/chem_search/ppls/082859-00002-20250512.pdf)

**US10194657B2 — Antimicrobial compositions.** Priority **2013-09-19**; filed **2014-09-19** as **14/491,358**; application publication **US20150079203A1**, **2015-03-19**; grant **2019-02-05**. Inventors: **Joseph Paul Thomas, Zsolt Istvan Hertelendy, Michael Lisle Howell**. The record initially identifies individuals, then Skirdle LLC, with a **2024-04-24** name-change assignment to **The Gilla Company, LLC**. Claim 1 requires specified concentrations of EDTA, 1,2-hexanediol, sodium dodecyl sulfate and a selected biocidal terpenoid in water. Dependent claims 4–5 recite at least **99.99% S. aureus efficacy up to 5 and 12 days** after application. [Patent text](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10194657B2/en)

Its persistence experiments coated sterile 12-well plates with 100 µL, allowed evaporation for 24 hours through 12 days, then added bacterial suspension for a 10-minute exposure. This supports laboratory persistence for the tested formulations, not worn/washed field surfaces or standard Purgo diluted 1:32. The text discusses cell lysis and, for certain bleach mixtures, possible ingredient stabilization; it does not establish the proposed bio-based protective barrier. [Patent examples and Table 2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10194657B2/en)

**US10813353B2 — Antimicrobial compositions.** Same **2013-09-19** priority and inventors; continuation filed **2019-02-04**, application **16/266,560**; grant **2020-10-27**. Original assignee shown: **Skirdle LLC**; retrieved current assignee: **The Gilla Company, LLC**. Claim 1 specifies EDTA, a straight-chain 1,2-alkanediol selected from pentanediol/hexanediol, a surfactant and carvacrol in water. Claims 4–5 also recite **5- and 12-day S. aureus efficacy**. Patent text contains no Purgo brand name; the EPA label supplies the product link. [Patent text](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10813353B2/en)

**Interpretation:** Persistence evidence exists in Purgo N's patent family. Patent claim scope, example formulation, marketed-product composition, and permitted label claims remain separate questions. These records do not authorize adding a 96-hour residual claim to either product's marketing. The N label identifies only its active ingredient percentage; it does not disclose enough other-ingredient detail to equate the marketed product with every patent example. Current assignment and enforceability remain unverified through the official patent docket.

## Purgo-specific interpretation

1. Purgo N now has an official EPA-label-to-patent crosswalk. Standard Purgo still requires its own patent number and SKU/formulation linkage.
2. Purgo N's antimicrobial patents contain laboratory persistence examples and multi-day dependent claims. The separate Green Products patents establish acid, base, concrete-cleaning, well-treatment and coating disclosures. Neither group establishes standard-Purgo 96-hour recolonization inhibition, a bio-based protective barrier, or field efficacy at 1:32 or 1:16.
3. EMS's own technology page supports a distinction between **Purgo surface cleaner** and **Purgo AF hand sanitizer**. Its separate **Purgo N** product must also remain distinct. Patent search results for AeroClean Pūrgo air-purification devices and Purgo Biologics dental products concern unrelated businesses.
4. Required missing evidence for standard Purgo: exact patent publication/grant numbers; current applicant/owner/license relationship; patent-to-formulation crosswalk for the marketed SKU; current label/TDS/SDS; complete residual-efficacy report with dilution, surfaces, organisms, contact time, re-inoculation, wear/wash conditions and endpoint. None should be inferred from Purgo N or an unrelated patent's title or general safety language.

## Search and verification limits

Searched EMS's exact corporate name, Purgo, SynTech, SynClean, related assignee name variants, and John MacDonald inventor variants; inspected the above patent texts, dates, claims, related applications and public legal-event entries. Follow-up inspection used patent numbers explicitly identified by the official Purgo N label. Official USPTO records substantiate the EMS/Green Products relationship and PTAB outcome; the official EPA label substantiates the Purgo N product-to-patent connection. Published patent text was read through Google Patents and its document mirrors; Google is not the issuing office and explicitly disclaims legal-status accuracy. Direct USPTO Patent Center/full-patent endpoints were not readable through this research client. Reference links are supplied for manual official-docket follow-up.

“Latest located” means newest relevant public record found in this bounded search, not certification of a complete worldwide portfolio, absence of unpublished applications, current ownership, fee-payment status or enforceability. Search-engine crawl dates were not treated as document publication dates.
