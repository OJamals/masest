The source also contains limitations that must accompany its favorable ratings:

- [Page 1](/Users/omar/Desktop/masest/purgo/Purgo-SDS-1-1-scaled.webp) lists a Warning signal word, possible harm if swallowed, mild skin irritation, and eye irritation. Thus 0-0-0 does not mean the document makes an unconditional absence-of-hazard claim.
- [Page 2](/Users/omar/Desktop/masest/purgo/Purgo-SDS-1-2-scaled.webp) advises avoiding vapors/mist and ensuring ventilation during release response.
- [Page 3](/Users/omar/Desktop/masest/purgo/Purgo-SDS-1-3-scaled.webp) says normal ventilation is generally adequate and respiratory protection is not required in its general guidance, while also directing avoidance of vapor/mist inhalation. It gives no occupied-space fogging or electrostatic exposure protocol and says not to let product enter storm drains.
- Page 5 reports no inhalation data and describes incomplete toxicological investigation. This is an evidence gap, not evidence of inhalation injury or proof of safety.
- [Page 6](/Users/omar/Desktop/masest/purgo/Purgo-SDS-1-6-768x994.jpg) lists DOT/IMDG/IATA/TDG non-dangerous-goods classifications with package/mode qualifications. It reports no persistence/degradability data. That ecological heading concerns environmental fate, not four-day antimicrobial efficacy.
- [Page 7](/Users/omar/Desktop/masest/purgo/Purgo-SDS-1-7-768x994.jpg) contains general regulatory statements; it does not identify a Purgo N pesticide registration or establish a current brand registration.

**Updated conclusion:** a historical EMS-attributed Purgo SDS is now available for comparison. The remaining gap is an authenticated current master SDS matched to the sold VertKleen formula/revision. Preserve this set as source evidence; do not silently rebrand it as a newly issued 2026 SDS.

## PDFs and text: compare identity before transferring claims

The [PDF comparison appendix](purgo-local-pdf-comparison-2026-09-25.md) records all seven PDF results, including byte identity with current public assets, printed dates versus file metadata, and the scope of the base-data tables.

The decisive distinctions are:

- `Purgo1.pdf` is byte-identical to the current manufacturer-linked standard-Purgo label. `MASEST Purgo Label.pdf` retains its ingredients and directions while changing the supplier block.
- `Purgo.pdf` is a different, historical CleanseItAll brochure. Its general starting dilution of 50% and apply/wait/rinse language conflict with treating it as the current task-specific label.
- `Purgo Bacterial Persist Test.pdf` is byte-identical to the current upstream 2020 persistence PDF. `PurgoN+Label.pdf` is also identical to the manufacturer's publicly linked N asset.
- The MASEST label, base-data PDF, Purgo 101, and persistence file exactly match the original-source hashes already recorded in the repository. Their reappearance does not add new validation. The base-data document's printed June 2025 heading and later file-modification metadata do not identify a new test of the current finished SKU.

The [RTF note](/Users/omar/Desktop/masest/purgo/purgo.rtf) is marketing prose invoking Xtreme and SynTech. It has no source URL, revision, ingredient list, or test method. The [plain-text note](</Users/omar/Desktop/masest/purgo/untitled text 2.txt>) describes EcoTivClean's Israeli EMS representation and local-market disinfecting claims, without the underlying approval identifier or current status. Neither text supplies a current US registration or a formulation crosswalk.

## Effect on the earlier recommendations

1. **Identity and page structure:** surface odor removal is explicitly supported by the supplied SDS. Keep surface applications prominent; reconcile the package and water-treatment directions.
2. **HMIS and transport:** stronger historic Purgo-specific documentation now exists. Keep the SDS's qualifications and obtain current revision confirmation before replacement publication.
3. **Dilution and economics:** use the actual manufacturer/MASEST labels and resolve conflicts; the infographic and prose notes cannot establish 1:16 or change the ratio convention.
4. **Residual efficacy:** distinguish the unchanged standard-Purgo study from Purgo N's separate patent family. A new copy of an old study is not new validation.
5. **Misting/fogging, hands, and regulatory claims:** no new method-specific exposure protocol or product-specific authorization is established by these images. The generic SDS guidance, foreign-market prose, and broad infographic claims cannot be combined into one unrestricted use claim.
