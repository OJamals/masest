# VertKleen Neutral: source research

Research date: 2026-09-25. Scope: Neutral / EMS SynClean N. Read-only external research and supplied-document review; no publication or manufacturer contact.

## Product identity and useful positioning

Neutral belongs to the SynClean N branch: neutral-pH cleaning and degreasing, distinct from high-foaming SynClean HD and low-foaming SynClean LF. EMS still links a dedicated N technical sheet from its [Industrial Cleaning page](https://www.enviromfg.com/industrial-cleaning). The current [SynClean N TDS](https://www.enviromfg.com/s/syncleannwso-tds.pdf) is byte-identical to `/Users/omar/Desktop/masest/synclean/syncleannwso-tds.pdf`: 2,385,153 bytes; SHA-256 `dcf52d198e66611316787961537a6e648963878abd16defb4b295eeb2fd9a9eb`. It carries ©2022; current availability does not mean a new 2026 revision.

Lead with **neutral-pH grease removal for everyday equipment, floor, and facility cleaning**. Supported selling points from the supplied VertKleen Neutral TDS: solvent-free, non-butyl, lifting petroleum oils and animal/vegetable fats, protein soils, non-emulsifying action, cold- or hot-water use, mild soapy odor, no VOCs, non-flammable, and 0-0-0 HMIS. These give buyers concrete reasons to replace solvent-based routine cleaners without claiming every competing product contains solvents.

Practical applications include shop floors, parts, vehicles, restaurant grease and general maintenance. Do not position Neutral as a scale remover or a direct replacement for concentrated high-pH process chemistry. EMS's [technology page](https://www.enviromfg.com/our-juice2) discusses the broader high-pH SynClean platform; that platform's sodium-hydroxide equivalence is not a Neutral dosing or performance claim.

## Dosing and packaging conflict

| Source | Printed directions |
|---|---|
| VertKleen Neutral PDF label | Floors 40:1; general cleaning 30:1; degreasing 10:1; heavy degreasing 3:1. Spray, sponge, brush or mop; brush and rinse with fresh water; repeat as needed. |
| New-studio PNG label | Light duty 1:20; general 1:10; heavy duty 1:5. Apply, agitate as needed, rinse. |
| EMS/VertKleen N TDS examples | Hydraulic fluid 5:1; fry hood/milk residue 10:1; carpets/vehicles/floors 30:1; windows 100:1. The dilution-specification field directs the reader to the product label. |

The PNG also says one gallon makes up to five gallons, which does not match its stated ratios if they mean one part concentrate plus water. It advertises 1,000–1,500 sq ft without a defined dilution/application basis. Do not derive an RTU price, coverage promise, or tank-dose calculator from this unresolved set.

**Recommended page treatment:** clear application steps and a dilution-guidance CTA; route buyers to the supplied product label and their actual container directions. Do not silently choose one label revision and present it as the current universal dose. If publishing the PDF's ratios, identify them explicitly as that supplied label's reference and resolve the packshot conflict separately. Flag the existing rendered packaging for corrected pH, ratios, yield, coverage, and sensitive-seal wording. Do not manufacture a new label through image generation.

Use “neutral pH” in primary copy. A technical reference can attribute pH 7.5 to the EMS SynClean N sheet; the new label's exact pH 7 conflicts. The SDS gives no pH data and cannot resolve the discrepancy.

## Machine use, materials, and handling

The supplied Neutral TDS permits rotary machines, steam-cleaning machines, and parts washers, but does not establish a low-foam specification. Offer CR HD Low Foam when foam control is the deciding requirement. Neither this research nor the provided N sheet establishes a compatibility matrix for every coating, elastomer, seal, or HVAC coil. Replace universal “safe on all seals” language with specific jobs and a material/application guidance CTA.

The Neutral label and SDS identify mild skin irritation, eye irritation, and potential harm if swallowed. SDS section VII says avoid skin/eye contact and mist, and prevent storm-drain release; section XIII routes surplus/non-recyclable solutions to a licensed disposal company. The TDS's biodegradability description does not establish that grease-laden wash water can be discharged anywhere. Keep practical instructions short: apply, agitate, rinse, recover wash water through the facility's existing process.

HMIS 0-0-0 is supported by the SDS. Section XIV lists non-regulated hazardous-material status for US DOT, Canadian TDG, IMO and IATA, with packaging/mode qualification; rail is not evaluated. Good buyer wording: non-hazmat shipping profile. Do not turn this into guaranteed cheaper freight or elimination of worker injury risk.

## Certifications, studies, and patents

The supplied N TDS lists NSF categories A1, A2, A3, A4, A5, A8, C1, C2 and D1; USDA A1, A2, A4, A8 and C3; a GRAS statement; and manufacturer statements about non-toxicity, non-mutagenicity, skin irritation and biodegradability. These are TDS summaries, not individual laboratory reports or current certificate documents. No Neutral-specific current certificate or underlying study report was located. Do not reinterpret D1 as a disinfectant claim or add pathogen efficacy.

The official [NSF letter for Renovo HD](https://info.nsf.org/USDA/letters/148038.pdf) concerns registration 148038, categories A1/K2. It is a different named product; no Neutral crosswalk found. Do not transfer that registration or its mark to Neutral.

EMS's N TDS says its formulation meets or exceeds Safer Choice requirements. The [EPA certified-product search](https://www.epa.gov/saferchoice/products), updated September 22, 2026, was inspected; no exact Neutral/SynClean N certificate was established by this research. Keep manufacturer attribution distinct from an independently verified current EPA certificate.

The [EMS patent page](https://www.enviromfg.com/patents) currently exposes a request form, not a product-to-patent list. The existing project dossier identifies the synthetic-base family US9399589B2 / US10368567B2 / US11311036B2, but this turn did not establish a Neutral-specific formulation crosswalk. The current Google Patents request for the last number failed in the browser tool. Do not present any family number as Neutral's proven patent or describe that patent's ingredients as Neutral's formulation. Manufacturer-level patented-technology wording remains attributable to EMS.

## Distributor ideas worth adapting

[EMS Slovenia](https://www.ems-slo.si/synclean-visoko-aktivno-sredstvo-za-ciscenje-in-razmascevanje) explicitly separates HD/high foam, LF/low foam and N/neutral pH. Its useful merchandising idea is choosing the formula by the job; its HD approvals, shelf life and dilution details do not transfer to Neutral.

[Vishes Global Automation](https://www.vishesglobalautomation.com/synclean-hd-industrial-degreaser.html) also mentions the neutral variant while grouping parts cleaning, equipment, floors and food-service grease by application. Borrow that clear job grouping, then substantiate each Neutral claim with the N records above.

Suggested conversion sequence: neutral-pH grease removal → equipment/floors/food-service jobs → apply/agitate/rinse steps → solvent-free and transport facts → supplied documents → task-specific sample request. No Neutral-only field result was established; do not relabel the HD kitchen or Walmart material as a Neutral case study.
