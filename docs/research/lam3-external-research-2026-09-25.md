## Editorial decisions

Lead with exterior stain removal, low-pressure application, bleach/quat/peroxide-free formulation, and a choice of finish schedules. Give buyers application steps, current-label dilution notation, genuine photos, technical sources, and a focused application/SDS request. Compare concentrate, labor, access, rinse work, repeat visits, and finished area instead of promising one result time for every property.

Distributor exploration found repeated EMS descriptions rather than stronger independent measurement. No reseller-only performance number was promoted into a fact. Synthetic-looking application imagery and conflicting packshots were withdrawn and recorded in the image replacement queue. No source PDFs, document hashes, certifications, or prices were changed.
