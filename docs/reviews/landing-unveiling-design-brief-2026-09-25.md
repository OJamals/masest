# VertKleen: product unveiling design brief

The user approved the simpler landing-page direction and requested an unveiling of the future: better chemistry, better results, less damage, lower costs, and products that feel almost too good to be true.

Design intent: make that surprise credible through a confident product presentation and immediate access to real evidence. Keep the approved sequence: stable hero → cleaning-job selection → proof → support → contact.

Use the existing Satoshi typography, restrained teal, near-black and white, authentic VertKleen packaging, and documented field photography. The primary action is “Find your cleaner”; the secondary is “See the results.”

Explore three independent visual directions:

- **The New Standard:** dark, theatrical product launch; centered headline and one controlled studio composition.
- **Material Intelligence:** bright, precise, asymmetric engineering presentation; product and material detail balanced against bold copy.
- **Ready for the Real World:** editorial, typography-led presentation; clear customer challenge, authentic product lineup, direct route to field results.

Keep the future-facing tone grounded in the actual products. Do not invent numeric savings, safety guarantees, certifications, customers, or cleaning evidence. Surface care and whole-job value are organizing themes; specific claims need product-specific support in implementation.

No six-scene scroll opener, changing purchase targets, scroll-driven photo wipes, repeated proof summaries, or hidden paragraph reveals. Product imagery in concept mockups is art direction, not verified cleaning evidence or a production asset.

The three generated mockups are visual selection material. Implementation follows selection of a visual target; existing application files remain untouched during ideation.
