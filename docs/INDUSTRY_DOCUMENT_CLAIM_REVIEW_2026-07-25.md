# Industry Document Claim Review — 2026-07-25

## Scope and decision rule

This sidecar inventories the exact 23-PDF set recorded as `claim_review_required` when this review began. The controlled ledger now records an explicit disposition for every file: 18 `restricted`, three `reference_only`, and two `resource_only`. None of those dispositions approves or substantiates a claim.

The controlled-document cover establishes distribution approval only. It is not technical, regulatory, legal, customer, OEM, laboratory, or certification substantiation. Objective claims require a reasonable basis before dissemination under the [FTC Advertising Substantiation Policy Statement](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/ftc-policy-statement-regarding-advertising-substantiation).

Disposition meanings:

- **Public technical data only** — may remain publicly accessible as a safety/handling reference, but must not be presented as industry proof or claim substantiation.
- **Remove from public industry proof** — remove from public proof/resource placement; retain internally if operationally necessary.
- **Requires named legal-technical approval** — restrict public distribution until an identified technical owner and legal/regulatory approver sign against traceable evidence for the exact product and claims.
- **Retain with qualification** — no document met this threshold. A disclaimer cannot cure unsupported objective claims.

Public-use shorthand: **I** = industry routes; **P** = product page; **R** = resource room; **C** = product catalog.

## Shared claim guardrails

1. **Evidence must precede publication.** For every objective safety, performance, environmental, comparative, certification, or customer claim, retain the exact SKU/formulation, lot, sponsor, test facility, protocol and revision, controls, conditions, raw or auditable results, acceptance criteria, report ID/date, and authorized signature. The FTC requires the advertised level of support where advertising states or implies that testing proves a claim ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/ftc-policy-statement-regarding-advertising-substantiation)).

2. **Do not convert transport or hazard classifications into broad safety claims.** PHMSA Class 8 determinations concern skin destruction and/or metal corrosion under transport rules ([49 CFR §173.136](https://www.phmsa.dot.gov/regulations/title49/section/173136)); they do not establish “safe,” “non-toxic,” universally noncorrosive, no-PPE, or no-downtime use. OSHA hazard communication requires classification, labels, SDSs, training, and workplace controls; an SDS is not evidence that PPE is unnecessary ([OSHA 29 CFR 1910.1200](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1200)).

3. **Keep method conclusions within method scope.** OECD 202 is a Daphnia acute immobilization test that reports an EC50, not a rat oral LD50 ([OECD 202](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/test-no-202-daphnia-sp-acute-immobilisation-test_9789264069947-en.html)). OECD 404 addresses dermal irritation/corrosion ([OECD 404](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/test-no-404-acute-dermal-irritation-corrosion_9789264242678-en.html)); OECD 471 addresses bacterial reverse mutation ([OECD 471](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/2020/06/test-no-471-bacterial-reverse-mutation-test_g1gh295b.html)). NIOSH 7903 measures inorganic acids, not carbon-dioxide generation ([NIOSH Method 7903 index](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/docs/2003-154/method-8000.html)). Hach Method 8000 measures chemical oxygen demand ([Hach](https://www.hach.com/p-cod-digestion-vials-ultra-low-range-1-to-40-mgl-cod-pk25/2415825)); it is not by itself a ready-biodegradability test. OECD 301 requires a defined aerobic biodegradation protocol, controls, and pass criteria ([OECD 301](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/1992/07/test-no-301-ready-biodegradability_g1gh2913.html)).

4. **Treat certification as exact-product evidence.** Record the current listing/certificate, certificate holder, exact trade name and SKU, formulation/private-label equivalence, approved categories and use limits, issue/expiry dates, and mark-use permission. The official NSF product directories are the controlling public lookup ([NSF certified products](https://www.nsf.org/certified-products-systems)). Exact trade-name searches for [WaterSafe60](https://info.nsf.org/Certified/PwsChemicals/Listings.asp?TradeName=WaterSafe60&Standard=060&) and [VertKleen](https://info.nsf.org/Certified/PwsChemicals/Listings.asp?TradeName=VertKleen&Standard=060&) returned no matching NSF/ANSI/CAN 60 product during this review; that absence is not proof that no private-label certification exists, so a certificate and equivalence record are required. The NSF nonfood letters located for Environmental Manufacturing Solutions identify **Elevate** ([A1, registration 148037](https://info.nsf.org/USDA/letters/148037.pdf)) and **Renovo HD** ([A1/K2, registration 148038](https://info.nsf.org/USDA/letters/148038.pdf)), not the VertKleen trade names in these PDFs.

5. **Do not infer Safer Choice certification from ingredients.** EPA evaluates the complete product and only certified products may carry the Safer Choice label ([EPA Safer Choice FAQ](https://www.epa.gov/saferchoice/frequently-asked-questions-safer-choice); [certified product search](https://www.epa.gov/saferchoice/products)). No exact VertKleen, MASEST, WaterSafe60, or MultiWash match was found in the [EPA Safer Choice public dataset](https://data.epa.gov/efservice/t_safer_choice_and_design_for_the_environment/JSON) during this review. Treat that as a retrieval result, not proof of non-certification; require an exact-product certificate.

6. **Antimicrobial and minimum-risk claims need a claim-by-claim regulatory file.** “Kills,” “sanitizes,” “disinfects,” and organism-control claims generally establish pesticidal intent ([EPA antimicrobial registration](https://www.epa.gov/pesticide-registration/antimicrobial-pesticide-registration)). A FIFRA 25(b) product must satisfy all six federal exemption conditions, and states may still require registration; labels also may not imply that a product is safe or non-toxic merely because it qualifies for the exemption ([EPA minimum-risk conditions](https://www.epa.gov/minimum-risk-pesticides/conditions-minimum-risk-pesticides)). Food and food-contact uses separately require a tolerance or exemption for every ingredient and intended use ([EPA tolerances](https://www.epa.gov/minimum-risk-pesticides/need-tolerances-and-tolerance-exemptions-minimum-risk-pesticides)).

7. **GRAS is not product approval.** FDA GRAS status concerns a substance under specified intended conditions of use; it does not establish that a formulated cleaning product is broadly FDA-approved, non-toxic, safe for incidental food contact, or suitable without rinse controls ([FDA GRAS](https://www.fda.gov/food/food-ingredients-packaging/generally-recognized-safe-gras)).

8. **Customer, agency, and OEM names require direct records.** Require an authorization or qualification record on the named entity’s letterhead that identifies the exact product/formulation, tested task and conditions, result, approver, date/term, and permission for public use. A specification reference alone is insufficient; for example, Boeing D6-17487 is a specific cleaning-solvent item/specification ([Boeing product data](https://shop.boeing.com/cpd/bdsis4_d6-17487)).

9. **Do not repurpose unrelated standards as product endorsements.** ISO 14064-1 addresses organization-level greenhouse-gas inventories and reporting ([ISO](https://www.iso.org/standard/66453.html)); ASTM E2129 is a data-collection practice for sustainability assessment of building products ([ASTM](https://store.astm.org/e2129-18.html)). Neither, without a directly applicable audited record, substantiates cleaning efficacy, chemical safety, food contact, no-PPE use, or customer savings.

10. **Keep generated imagery separate from field proof.** Generated scenes may illustrate a cleaning task only and must be labeled and governed as illustrative content. Field proof requires source capture, permission, site/date/task, exact product and dilution, dwell time, application/rinse/mechanical action, before/after chain of custody, result, and limitations. A generated image, stock image, testimonial, or brochure table is never primary field evidence.

11. **Clearance must be named and traceable.** No `claim_review_required` status should change until both a named technical owner and named legal/regulatory approver sign the exact claim set and cite its evidence package. Customer/OEM claims also require written public-use authorization from the named party.
